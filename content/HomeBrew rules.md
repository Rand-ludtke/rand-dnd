### **Call Shot**

Take disadvantage(dm's discretion) on a shot to call where you hit an enemy. This will cause special effects like blinding an opponent, tripping them, lose of limb etc. 

### **Poison making**

You can Halve the volume of poison to increase any save DC by 1 up to 20 (50ml per arrow dose 200ml for melee weapon or like) the opposite is the same meaning double volume of poison with water decrease save DC by 1.

### **Gambling**

you draw a Tarot card or roll a d100(real one(aka ball)) to get a effect at dm's discretion 

### **Pets**

your pets and or kids won't be targeted in battle as long as they don't fight

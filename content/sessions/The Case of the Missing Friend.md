### **Hook: The Guild Quest Poster**

The party finds a quest poster pinned to the Adventurer’s Guild bulletin board. The poster reads:

> **HELP WANTED: FIND MY FRIEND!**  
> _My dear friend, Elara Voss, has gone missing! She’s a novice inventor who lives in the Gadget District. She’s been gone for three days, and I’m worried something terrible has happened. Please investigate her home at 17 Gearspark Lane and find her! Reward: 500 gold pieces and a mysterious invention of her creation._  
> — _Sincerely, Clara Brightspark_

Clara Brightspark is Elara’s childhood friend and a local herbalist. She’s not an inventor herself but has always supported Elara’s dreams. Clara is worried because Elara has never disappeared for this long without telling her.

---

### **Step 1: Meeting Clara Brightspark**

Before heading to Elara’s home, the party can visit Clara at her herbalist shop, _Brightspark Botanicals_, located in the Merchant District. Clara is a kind, slightly nervous woman in her mid-20s with a green apron and a faint smell of lavender.

- **Clara’s Information:**
    
    - Elara is a novice tinkerer who dreams of becoming the apprentice of **Tink Thunderclank**, a famous gnome inventor known for his groundbreaking work in automata.
        
    - Elara has been working on a "revolutionary energy core" to impress Tink, but Clara thinks it might be too dangerous.
        
    - Elara was last seen three days ago, muttering something about "final tests" and "warehouse safety protocols."
        
    - Clara gives the party the key to Elara’s home and begs them to find her friend.
        

---

### **Step 2: Arriving at Elara’s Home**

Elara’s house is a modest three-story townhouse at **17 Gearspark Lane** in the Gadget District. The house looks slightly lopsided, with a chimney that emits occasional puffs of smoke. A sign above the door reads _"Voss Innovations: Enter at Your Own Risk!"_ The front door is locked, but Clara’s key opens it.

- **Outside Observations:**
    
    - The windows are covered with thick curtains.
        
    - A small, mechanical bird sits on the roof, chirping intermittently.
        
    - A faint smell of burnt metal lingers in the air.
        

---

### **Step 3: Investigating the House**

The interior of the house is cluttered with half-finished inventions, blueprints, and strange gadgets. The party must navigate carefully, as many of Elara’s inventions double as accidental traps.

#### **Ground Floor: The Workshop**

- **Description:**  
    The workshop is filled with gears, wires, and strange contraptions. A large workbench dominates the room, covered in tools and blueprints. A mechanical cat (non-hostile) watches the party from a shelf.
    
- **Traps and Hazards:**
    
    - **The Roomba Mine:** A small, circular device whirs around the floor, cleaning up debris. It looks suspiciously like a landmine. If disturbed or tampered with, it explodes (DC 15 Dexterity saving throw, 3d6 fire damage, half on success).
        
    - **Flying Wrenches:** A tripwire (DC 14 Perception to spot) triggers a spring-loaded mechanism that flings wrenches across the room (2d6 bludgeoning damage).
        
    - **Sticky Floor:** A spilled vial of adhesive has created a sticky patch (DC 12 Acrobatics to avoid being stuck for 1 round).
        
- **Clues:**
    
    - A half-finished blueprint on the workbench shows a design for a "Personal Defense Drone."
        
    - A note reads: _"Clara, I think I’ve finally cracked the code for the self-sustaining energy core! But I need to test it in Warehouse 7 by the docks. If I don’t return in two days, something’s gone wrong. —Elara."_
        

#### **Second Floor: The Living Quarters**

- **Description:**  
    The living room is cozy but cluttered. A mechanical teapot on the table occasionally spouts steam. A bookshelf is filled with titles like _"Advanced Clockwork Engineering"_ and _"The Art of Explosive Diplomacy."_
    
- **Traps and Hazards:**
    
    - **Exploding Cushions:** Sitting on the couch triggers a hidden mechanism that causes the cushions to explode (DC 13 Dexterity saving throw, 2d8 fire damage, half on success).
        
    - **Animated Rug:** The rug is enchanted to attack intruders (treat as a Rug of Smothering).
        
- **Clues:**
    
    - A journal on the table details Elara’s experiments with a dangerous energy core. The last entry reads: _"The core is unstable, but I can’t stop now. If I can harness its power, it could revolutionize everything! Tink Thunderclank will HAVE to take me as his apprentice!"_
        

#### **Third Floor: The Laboratory**

- **Description:**  
    The laboratory is filled with bubbling beakers, glowing crystals, and a large, humming device in the center of the room. The device has a cracked energy core leaking sparks.
    
- **Traps and Hazards:**
    
    - **Energy Core Overload:** The cracked core emits random bursts of energy (DC 15 Dexterity saving throw, 3d8 lightning damage, half on success).
        
    - **Falling Shelves:** A loose shelf collapses if disturbed (DC 12 Dexterity saving throw, 2d6 bludgeoning damage).
        
- **Clues:**
    
    - A scrap of paper on the floor reads: _"Warehouse 7, Dock District. Final test location. Danger: High."_
        

---

### **Step 4: The Warehouse Showdown**

The party heads to **Warehouse 7** in the Dock District, where they find Elara clinging to a chandelier high above the ground. The warehouse is filled with malfunctioning inventions, including a large, spider-like construct and several smaller drones patrolling the area.

#### **Elara’s Situation:**

- Elara explains that she came to the warehouse to test her energy core, but it overloaded and caused her inventions to go haywire. She’s been trapped for days, dodging her own creations.
    
- She managed to escape onto the chandelier but can’t get down without help.
    

#### **Enemies:**

- **Malfunctioning Spider Construct** (Reskinned Animated Armor with spider climb and a ranged lightning attack).
    
- **4 Roomba Mines** (Reskinned Fire Beetles that explode on death, dealing 2d6 fire damage in a 5-foot radius).
    
- **2 Defense Drones** (Reskinned Flying Swords with ranged attacks).
    

#### **Objective:**

- Defeat the constructs and rescue Elara.
    
- Elara warns the party that the energy core is about to overload, giving them 3 rounds to escape before the warehouse explodes (10d10 fire damage to anyone inside).
    

---

### **Conclusion**

Once Elara is rescued, she thanks the party profusely and explains that she was trying to impress Tink Thunderclank with her energy core. She realizes now that she bit off more than she could chew and promises to be more careful in the future.

- **Rewards:**
    
    - 500 gold pieces.
        
    - A choice of one of Elara’s inventions:
        
        1. **Personal Defense Drone:** A small drone that follows the user and can make a ranged attack (1d8 lightning damage) once per short rest.
            
        2. **Sticky Bomb:** A single-use explosive that sticks to a target and deals 4d6 fire damage in a 10-foot radius.
            
        3. **Clockwork Owl:** A mechanical owl that can scout ahead and relay information to the party.
            
- **Future Hooks:**
    
    - Elara offers to provide the party with future discounts on her inventions.
        
    - Tink Thunderclank hears about the party’s heroics and seeks them out for a special job.

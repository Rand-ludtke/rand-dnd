    ### Side Quest: Night Raid Mystery

#### Quest Hook

The party hears of a series of nighttime raids on merchant stockpiles in the city. A wealthy merchant, **Valric Othmont**, has put out a request for adventurers to protect his goods from these supposed thieves. However, the raiders claim Valric is actually trafficking people, and they are trying to free them.

#### Key Characters

- **Valric Othmont (Human, Merchant)** – A wealthy, well-dressed trader with an air of arrogance. Claims innocence and insists the raiders are thieves trying to ruin his business.
    
- **Captain Edris Vale (Half-Orc, City Guard)** – A strict but fair officer in charge of investigating the robberies. Initially skeptical but willing to hear both sides.
    
- **Lina Farrow (Elf, Rebel Leader)** – The leader of the raiders, passionate and fierce. Claims to have seen firsthand evidence of Valric’s crimes.
    
- **Jakob “Rat” Trelven (Halfling, Informant)** – A streetwise rogue who has information about both Valric and Lina but requires a favor in return.
    

#### Steps for the Quest

1. **Receiving the Job**
    
    - The party hears about the raids through city rumors or a direct request from Valric.
        
    - They meet with Captain Edris Vale, who suggests caution before jumping to conclusions.
        
2. **Investigating the Situation**
    
    - Stake out Valric’s warehouse at night.
        
    - Follow the raiders or confront them mid-raid.
        
    - Gather information from Jakob “Rat” Trelven in exchange for a small favor.
        
3. **Choosing a Side**
    
    - Defend Valric’s warehouse and fight off the raiders.
        
    - Help the raiders expose Valric’s crimes.
        
    - Search for hard evidence to determine the truth.
        
4. **Confrontation and Resolution**
    
    - If they side with Valric, the raiders flee or are arrested.
        
    - If they side with Lina, they expose Valric and may face consequences from the city guard.
        
    - If they investigate, they may find definitive proof leading to a final confrontation.
        

#### Possible Outcomes

- **Side with Valric**: Defend his stock, fight off the raiders, and report them to the guards. Gain wealth but risk aiding a criminal if he is guilty.
    
- **Side with Lina**: Help expose Valric, possibly raiding his warehouse. Gain favor with local rebels but risk conflict with the city guard.
    
- **Investigate Further**: Seek more evidence before choosing a side. Talking to Jakob or Captain Edris could reveal more information.
    

#### Challenges & Encounters

- **Stealth & Investigation**: Sneaking into Valric’s warehouse or tracking the raiders’ movements.
    
- **Combat**: Defending or attacking a caravan, or a skirmish between the two sides.
    
- **Social Encounters**: Persuading Captain Edris, negotiating with Lina, or uncovering information from Jakob.

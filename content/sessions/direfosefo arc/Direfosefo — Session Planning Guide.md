## **Session Structure**

### Phase 1: Arrival & Initial Exploration

**Locations:** High Altitude Brews, Tink’s Curiosities, Shimmering Pool, Whispering Grotto

**Goals:**

- Introduce town and daily life.
    
- Plant subtle clues without revealing full infernal plot.
    
- Encourage social interaction and roleplay.
    

**Clues & Checks:**

- **High Altitude Brews:**
    
    -  Hidden ledger of infernal deliveries.
        
    - Patsy’s unconscious infernal humming.
        
- **Tink’s Curiosities:**
    
    - Detect cursed or greed-inducing trinkets.
        
    - Manager admits ignorance of shipment sources (DC 18 Persuasion).
        
- **Shimmering Pool:**
    
    - Fish behavior changes.
        
    - Detect faint infernal energy.
        
    - Patterns in algae growth.
        
- **Whispering Grotto:**
    
    - Hidden chamber and infernal crystal.
        
    - Caretaker is nervous or hiding something.
        

**GM Notes:**

- Clues should be partial: e.g., noticing discoloration, unusual whispers, or strange fish behavior without immediately pointing to infernal involvement.
    
- Encourage players to talk to townsfolk; responses are carefully controlled to mislead them toward Dragon Cult rumors.
    

---

### Phase 2: Town Hall & Guard Hall Investigation

**Locations:** Town Hall, Guard Hall & Prison

**Goals:**

- Introduce administrative and guard structures.
    
- Place clues of infernal contracts and hidden operations.
    
- Increase DCs to reflect devil mastery.
    

**Clues & Checks:**

- Hidden mayor office, altered files.
    
- Secret tunnel entrance or strange activity.
    
- Hesitant guards or microexpressions.
    
- Moving undetected in the secret tunnel.
    

**Session Hooks:**

- Party finds a partially burned ledger pointing to minor infernal contracts.
    
- Civilians report night-time activities: mysterious shadows, sounds of movement from Guard Hall.
    
- Allow players to slowly connect minor anomalies to major infernal influences.
    

---

### Phase 3: Direfosefo Market & Shops

**Goals:**

- Expose infernal economic control.
    
- Introduce black market and hidden cultist activity.
    
- Encourage investigation and deduction.
    

**Clues & Checks:**

- Detect cursed items in shops.
    
- Merchants behaving strangely or unusually prosperous.
    
- Discover hidden alley or black market.
    
- Detect Dragon Cult symbols.
    
- Overhear suspicious conversations.
    

**Session Hooks:**

- Black market leads to minor infernal agent encounter or cursed item acquisition.
    
- Merchant anomalies slowly reveal the devil’s control over trade.
    
- Red herrings (Dragon Cult clues) nudge party away from infernal truth.
    

---

### Phase 4: Nearby Town Exploration (1–3 Days Travel)

**Nearby Towns:** Kirthaven, Mossfield, Larrow’s Point, Orlinstead, Coldmere, Yulder’s Gap, Twinbrook, Elensford, Shallowgrave, Grettan Hill, Windmere, Dovelight, Redwick, Flintgate, Oakshade

**Goals:**

- Continue subtle clue distribution.
    
- Introduce side plots, minor encounters, and environmental storytelling.
    
- Allow devils to misdirect the party with minor cult activity hints.
    

**General Checks & Clues:**

- Hidden cult symbols, unusual shipping crates, or abandoned structures.
    
- Minor magical anomalies or traces of infernal energy.
    
- Locals acting nervously, withholding information.
    
- Track suspicious travelers or shipments.
    

**Session Hooks:**

- Local rumors exaggerate Dragon Cult activity, nudging the party away from infernal schemes.
    
- Small events (vanishing livestock, strange lights) reinforce tension and curiosity.
    
- Optional: Minor encounters with infernal agents to subtly gauge the party’s actions.
    

---

## **Tips for Slow-Burn Investigation**

1. Reveal partial clues rather than complete information; make evidence circumstantial.
    
2. Adjust DCs to show devilish skill and oversight.
    
3. Encourage multiple small investigations rather than one big revelation.
    
4. Use environmental and NPC cues to misdirect focus toward Dragon Cult while infernal plot develops.
    
5. Seed the Alta family connections, especially for Lu Alta, through casual dialogue or observations in the café.
    
6. Track corruption progression in Shimmering Pool and Whispering Grotto, subtly showing escalation to build suspense.

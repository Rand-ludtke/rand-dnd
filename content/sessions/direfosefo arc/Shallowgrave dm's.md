> A peaceful, somber town built upon history's echo, where the people honor the past so thoroughly that they sometimes fail to look to the future.

#### **Overview**

- **Population:** ~500
    
- **Vibe:** Peaceful, respectful, and slightly melancholic. The air is clean and still. The people are calm, polite, and deeply traditional. They are farmers, artisans, and historians, not warriors. The town's morbid name is spoken with pride, not fear; it is a reminder of the cost of peace.
    
- **Theme:** Legacy and Memory. The entire identity of Shallowgrave is built around the historic battlefield it borders. This reverence for the past borders on stagnation, and the town is ill-prepared for a real, present-day threat.
    
- **Secret:** The battlefield is **not** entirely at rest. The intense bloodshed and forgotten dead from the battle left a minor **psychic resonance**. This doesn't create undead, but instead causes faint auditory hallucinations (the clash of steel, distant war cries) and a feeling of being watched on the field. A lonely, half-mad scholar, **Kaelen**, who lives on the edge of the field, is secretly trying to "amplify" this resonance to "hear the truth of history," unaware he is risking creating a much more dangerous phenomenon.

#### **Adventure Hooks & Quests**

- **The Unquiet Past:** Elder Moira is concerned about Kaelen. He's become more reclusive and strange. She asks the party to check on him, fearing for his health.
    
- **A Scholar's Request:** Kaelen himself excitedly approaches the party. He claims he is on the verge of a "historical breakthrough" and needs them to retrieve a specific, rare mineral from the nearby hills to "power his listening device."
    
- **The Caretaker's Concern:** Rook the caretaker has found strange, scorched marks on one of the central memorial stones that weren't there before. He wants the party to find out what—or who—made them.
    
- **A New Song:** Corwin the bard offers to pay the party if they can go out and have a real, new adventure he can write a song about. "Surely there's _something_ interesting around here besides that old field!"
    
- **The Veteran's Eye:** Gideon the retired soldier asks the party to walk the field with him. From a tactical standpoint, the official history of the battle "doesn't make sense." He wants to prove there's a missing piece to the story.

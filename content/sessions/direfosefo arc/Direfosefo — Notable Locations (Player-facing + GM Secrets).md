# [[High Altatude Brews]]

**Player-Facing Description (read aloud):**  
A warm, sunlit café perched near the lake’s edge. Wooden tables, hanging herb bunches, and the scent of roasted beans and fresh pastries make the air feel like a hug. The Altas—Patsy at the counter, Calluway in the back oven—move with the practiced rhythm of owners who love what they do. A neat herb garden and a stack of handwritten recipe cards sit by the back door.

**GM Notes & Secrets (do not show players):**

- The Alta family (Patsy & Calluway) _did_ sign a pact with infernal agents years ago to guarantee business success. They truly believed it was a one-time blessing and regret certain small clauses now, though they hide evidence and are ashamed. They are NOT actively evil, and they will protect Lu at cost to themselves.
    
- The Alta house has a secret compartment behind a loose floorboard beneath the pantry. Inside: a folded vellum contract (infernal script) and a small brass token stamped with the mayor’s sigil. **Discovery:** Investigation DC 18 to notice the loose board / Perception DC 17 to notice a faint draft; Investigation DC 20 to unseal the compartment and read the contract (or Arcana DC 15 to read infernal marks).
    
- The mayor visits the café at odd hours under the pretense of private meetings and always leaves with a sealed packet the Alta's swear is “bakery supplies.” He treats the Alta's gently, which only deepens their guilt when the party suspects him.
    
- Patsy will show visible signs of stress if pressured about the contract: she fidgets, avoids the mayor’s name, and sometimes hides money jars. Insight DC 18 to notice.
    

**Hooks / Scenes to Run:**

- A late-night pastry delivery: the party finds the mayor here after hours. He is polite and guarded; if the party is rude he’ll withdraw and later use the incident as a reason to increase guard presence.
    
- The secret compartment is _real_ — if found, reading the contract should trigger a minor infernal lure (a whisper or brief charm effect) unless a successful Wisdom save DC 19 is made.

# [[Tink’s Curiosities (Direfosefo Branch)]]

**Player-Facing Description:**  
A cramped shop of brass and glass: wind-up clockwork toys, labeled jars of shimmering dust, and a small counter with a bell. The branch owner is friendly and chatty, proud of their oddities.

**GM Notes & Secrets (do not show players):**

- The shop receives occasional shipments of unusual components: faintly-cold iron, red-tinted vial samples, and small carved tokens. These shipments are _sourced_ (indirectly) from a disguised infernal merchant who deals through middlemen.
    
- **Devil Merchant:** _Malkor Veyl_ (alias: "Malkor Vale", a traveling broker) is the hidden source. He poses as an exotic goods trader who only appears at market fairs or sends sealed crates through Marla Ashenbrook’s trade network. He marks his crates with an innocuous glyph (a little crescent with a dot) that normal merchants dismiss as a shipping brand.
    
    - **Ledger clue:** Marla's ledger lists purchases from "Vale & Sons" with a recurring odd phrase: _"Balance rendered in daylight."_ A successful Investigation or Insight DC 20 will flag it as unusual.
        
    - **Finding Malkor:** If the party spots Malkor at market, Perception DC 24 or Investigation DC 20 reveals his fine-laced clothing is slightly off (tiny infernal script embroidered under cuffs). Search or pickpocketing (DC 24 Sleight of Hand / Thieves’ Tools DC 24) on a crate can reveal a stamped crate corner with his sigil.
        
- Tink (or the branch manager) is unaware of the true origin. Many items sold are cursed/malfunctioning in subtle ways: a clock that runs backwards on stormy nights, a music box that hums an odd minor chord when a fire is lit nearby.
    

**GM Use:**

- If the PCs trace the goods, Malkor will never be a cannon-fodder encounter—he is careful. He will use bribes, misdirection, and hired hands; if forced, he might try to trade secrets for safety.

# [[The Whispering Grotto]]

**Player-Facing Description:**  
A narrow path leads under a rocky knuckle to an opening. Inside, the cavern walls break sound into shimmering echoes. Speak softly and listen: voices overlap like old songs, and people claim to hear answers to questions they didn’t ask.

**GM Secrets & Mechanics (do not show players):**

- **Hidden Control Chamber (the big secret):** Deep within the grotto a chamfered stone door covers a small chamber containing a crude acoustic mechanism: three aligned `whisper-crystals` embedded in carved sockets and a series of bronze tubes that direct sound. The device was installed centuries ago by a cult of sound-mages and later re-tuned by infernal agents to seed suggestions and false memories into echoes.
    
    - **Finding the chamber:** The grotto appears ordinary until someone _looks for it._ Use the following discovery ladder:
        
        - _Perception to notice oddities:_ Perception DC 24 notices a faint, regular wind and a steady hum behind the echoing wall.
            
        - _Investigation to find an entry seam:_ Investigation DC 26 locates a nearly invisible seam where stone has been shaved; DC 22 to work the hidden latch (or a DC 18 Strength check to pry the stone loose).
            
        - _Arcana check:_ Arcana DC 19 recognizes that the echoes have been magically tuned to push specific emotions (confidence, dread, nostalgia).
            
    - **Inside the chamber:** The whisper-crystals are small, translucent stones that resonate when struck by the stream of air; rotating one changes pitch and _content_ of whispers (the crystals are keyed to rhymed phonemes, not full sentences). Tampering with them requires Dexterity (Tinker) or Intelligence (Tinker/Arcana) checks: DC 15 to reroute and change the echo-target, DC 18 to silence it without damaging the mechanism
        
    - **Danger:** The crystals are bound with an infernal sigil — removing one without proper rites requires a Wisdom saving throw DC 14 or the user suffers a short bout of haunting whispers (disadvantage on the next social check). A devil with true sight can use the device to broadcast commands through the echoes (limited to suggestions, not direct compulsion).
        

**How devils use it:**

- They tune the grotto to drop occasional false-omens about the "dragon cult." The echoes will nudge investigators away from certain leads and toward others (e.g., mention a "red sigil by the water" that doesn’t exist).
    

**Plot Hook Idea:**

- The party hears a whispered warning for a "rite at the pool" — an echo that looks like evidence of cult activity. If the party investigates, the grotto’s device quietly reinforced the rumor.

# [[The Shimmering Pool]]

**Player-Facing Description:**  
A calm cove of the lake glitters at night. On certain nights, the water seems lit from below: scales of tiny fish glimmer like pinpricks of light and the whole bay gains an unearthly sheen.

**GM Secrets & Corruption Mechanics (do not show players):**

- The pool is being _slowly tainted_ by infernal influence tied into the mayor’s small ritual observances and shipments funneled through Marla and Malkor.
    
- The corruption is subtle and gradually intensifies. Use the following **corruption model** to pace escalation. Adjust any constants to suit your campaign speed.
    

### Corruption model (pick discrete or continuous)

**Discrete (simple; per full moon):**

- Let `n` = number of full moons since the first noticeable change (n = 0 on first observed red-tinged full moon).
    
- Corruption intensity `I(n)` = `min(1.0, I0 + r * n)`
    
    - `I0` = 0.10 (initial tinge on the first full moon)
        
    - `r` = 0.07 per full moon (growth per cycle — adjust between 0.03–0.15 for slower/faster escalation)
        
    - `I` is clamped to 1.0 (fully corrupted)
        

**Continuous (granular; days):**

- Let `t` = days since first event. Lunar period `T` = 29.5 days.
    
- `I(t) = I_max * (1 - exp(-r * (t / T))) * (1 + a * sin(2π * t / T))`
    
    - Example constants: `I_max` = 1.0, `r` = 0.20, `a` = 0.30
        
    - This produces a slowly rising baseline with predictable spikes near full moons.
        

**Event frequency (how often ‘minor corruption events’ happen):**

- Base frequency `f0` = 1 event every 90 days (0.011 events/day). Each full moon increases frequency by `s` = 0.15 events per moon.
    
- Discrete frequency after `n` moons: `f(n) = max(0.02, f0 + s * n)` (so it never drops below once every ~50 days). Adjust `s` smaller for very slow effects.
    

---

### Corruption thresholds & sample effects

(Use `I` to determine visible changes; `f` to determine how often oddities occur)

- **I < 0.2 — Early tinge (cosmetic):**
    
    - On full moons the pool glows a slightly reddened shimmer. Fish show faint reddish speckles. Locals shrug it off as "moonlight." No immediate danger.
        
    - _Clues:_ fishermen report "red flecks" in nets (Investigation DC 12 to spot abnormal coloration).
        
- **0.2 ≤ I < 0.5 — Noticeable change:**
    
    - The fish’s bioluminescence shifts redder; a few fish display odd symmetrical scales. Night fog near the shore sometimes carries the faint scent of brimstone on the wind.
        
    - _Clues:_ Arcana or Nature DC 14 recognizes unnatural reagents in water; Animal Handling DC 13 notices fish agitation.
        
- **0.5 ≤ I < 0.8 — Biological effects:**
    
    - Fish begin to show mild morphological changes (extra finlets, thicker scales), some become aggressive toward small animals. Local birds avoid feeding on the lake.
        
    - _Clues:_ Medicine DC 15 or Nature DC 15 to identify the mutations; Religion/Arcana DC 16 to detect infernal influence.
        
- **I ≥ 0.8 — Clear corruption:**
    
    - The pool emits a faint low hum at night. Fish with small horn-like growths are occasionally found. Local wildlife don’t drink the water. Townsfolk grow uneasy; devils press the narrative that a "cult must be active."
        
    - _Clues:_ Strong infernal signature (Arcana DC 18 / Religion DC 17). The mayor will propose a highly publicized cleansing ritual (a staged event).
        

**GM Note:** Tune `r` and `s` to match your preferred timeline. With `r=0.07` per moon the pool reaches `I≈0.92` in ~12 full moons (1 year). If you want this to be slower, drop `r` to 0.03.

# [[Town Hall & Guard Hall]] 

**Player-Facing Description:**

- Town Hall is clean, orderly, and welcoming. The mayor occasionally holds public meetings, shaking hands and giving speeches. The Guard Hall is adjacent: its front is polished and officers are present on duty, but the basement is restricted.
    

**GM Secrets:**

- **Mayor’s Hidden Operations:**
    
    - A secret office behind a sliding bookshelf exists in Town Hall (DC 24 Investigation or DC 18 with Detect Magic). Here, the mayor keeps infernal correspondences and ritual diagrams pointing to both the Shimmering Pool and the Whispering Grotto.
        
    - The office contains a small scrying crystal that records visitors and can relay information to infernal superiors.
        
- **Guard Hall Secret Tunnel:**
    
    - Basement has a narrow, hidden tunnel used for moving prisoners or discreet shipments. Investigation DC 16 to notice a trapdoor hidden under a rug. Stealth DC 26 needed to navigate undetected.
        
    - The tunnel leads to a concealed holding chamber with cages, some holding individuals who resisted infernal contracts. DC 15 Perception to notice faint magical chains embedded in walls for controlling captives.
        
- **Infernal Influence in Guards:**
    
    - Captain Thaldris Kael uses subtle fear and intimidation; some guards may be willing to cooperate if players notice microexpressions or hesitations (Insight DC 24).
        
    - Patrol schedules and logbooks are deliberately misleading; high Investigation (DC 17) or Insight (DC 16) can reveal inconsistencies.
        

**Clues & Checks Cheat Notes:**

- Investigation DC 22 → missing or altered official files
    
- Persuasion DC 19 → bribing clerks for hints
    
- Perception DC 26 → hidden tunnels
    
- Stealth DC 26 → moving through the tunnel unnoticed
    
- Insight DC 24 → hesitant guards or microexpressions of fear
    

**Session Hooks:**

- Party may find a hidden map showing potential infernal corruption sites (pool, grotto, Tink’s). A subtle nudge from Thaldris’ whispers may guide party to investigate cult-related rumors.
    
- Civilians sometimes report seeing unusual night activity at the Guard Hall (faint sounds, mysterious shadows), DC 13 Perception to spot evidence.
    
- A clerical error in Town Hall files could accidentally reveal minor contracts or past shipments; could serve as a slow burn clue for players.
    

**GM Tips:**

- Present these areas as functional and impressive to avoid immediate suspicion.
    
- Place clues gradually, allow checks to reveal only partial truths, reinforcing infernal misdirection.
    
- Use guard behaviors and administrative anomalies to seed suspicion without confirming full infernal presence immediately.

# Direfosefo Market & Additional Shops — GM Secrets & Mechanics

**Player-Facing Description:**

- A bustling hub of activity with stalls selling fresh produce, crafted goods, and local wares. Patrons haggle for the best prices while townsfolk share news and gossip. Shops line the streets offering blacksmithing, tailoring, potions, and general supplies.
    

**GM Secrets:**

- **Infernal Merchant Influence:**
    
    - Marla Ashenbrook, the wealthiest trader, secretly works with devils to control the flow of key goods. She ensures rare components and magical items appear only to certain buyers.
        
    - Some items sold in the market carry subtle enchantments that promote selfishness or minor misfortune (DC 15 Arcana to detect). These are particularly popular trinkets and charms.
        
- **Unseen Contracts:**
    
    - Certain merchants have unknowingly signed infernal contracts, giving the devils leverage over trade. Players might notice odd behavior or sudden wealth in NPCs (DC 16 Insight).
        
- **Black Market & Hidden Goods:**
    
    - A hidden alley behind the Rusted Horseshoe leads to an underground vendor dealing in cursed items and illicit magical components. Detecting this alley requires DC 17 Investigation.
        
- **Minor Cultist Activity:**
    
    - A few stalls are fronts for Dragon Cult sympathizers. Players might find minor clues (coded symbols, unusual trading patterns) that suggest cult activity if they look closely (DC 15 Investigation or DC 14 Arcana).
        

**Clues & Checks Cheat Notes:**

- DC 16 Arcana → detect minor cursed items or subtle enchantments.
    
- DC 16 Insight → notice merchants behaving oddly or unusually prosperous.
    
- DC 17 Investigation → discover hidden alley or black market activity.
    
- DC 17 Arcana → detect subtle symbols of the Dragon Cult in certain shops.
    
- DC 15 Perception → overhear whispered deals or odd conversations.
    

**Session Hooks:**

- Players may notice that some of the enchanted trinkets sold at Tink’s or in the market seem to influence townsfolk behavior.
    
- Strange delivery routes or shipment patterns may encourage investigation into the Mayor and Marla Ashenbrook.
    
- Black market alley could serve as a location to find a low-level infernal agent or minor cursed item, slowly revealing the devils’ influence.
    
- Cultist stalls provide red herrings; encourage players to spend time gathering clues to slowly uncover the true infernal plot.
    

**GM Tips:**

- Present the market as lively and safe to the players initially, gradually letting them notice anomalies.
    
- Use subtle behavioral clues to make devils’ influence obvious only to careful observers.
    
- Maintain the slow-burn tension by having multiple small clues rather than one obvious revelation.

## GM Tips — tone & pacing

- Keep the Altas sympathetic; they are victims of a bargain they regret and can be redemptive allies.
    
- Let devils steer, but never fully block. Give slow, believable clues that lead toward the cult—but make those clues _planted_. A careful party with high Arcana/Insight will catch the false signature earlier.
    
- Use the corruption model to schedule atmospheric beats between sessions: a small red tinge or weird fish report is an excellent one-line hook between sessions.

> A quiet, mist-shrouded town where the line between medicine and magic is as blurred as the horizon at dawn.

#### **Overview**

- **Population:** ~600
    
- **Vibe:** Peaceful, secluded, and slightly eerie. The air is thick with the smell of damp earth and a hundred different herbs. The townsfolk are friendly but reserved, with a deep, almost spiritual connection to the surrounding marsh and forest. They speak softly and move with purpose.
    
- **Theme:** Naturalism and Subconscious Fear. The town is a haven of healing and natural wisdom, but a psychic echo from a nearby fen has begun infesting the dreams of its inhabitants.
    
- **Secret:** An **Allip** (a spectral undead born from a soul that died obsessed with a forbidden truth) is trapped in the **Dreamer's Fen**. Its despairing whispers are seeping into the town's collective subconscious, causing the strange dreams. A local alchemist, **Kaelen**, is secretly trying to commune with the entity, believing its whispers hold the formula for a perfect healing elixir, unaware he is being driven mad.

#### **Adventure Hooks & Quests**

- **The Dreamer's Sketches:** Finn's parents are desperate. They'll pay the party to investigate the meaning behind their son's constant, eerie drawings. _Lead:_ The symbol matches a family crest in Petra's history books, pointing to the vanished scholar.
    
- **A Botanic Blight:** Mallow offers a reward for anyone who can figure out why his Moonpetals are dying. _Lead:_ The black sap is a psychic residue. Analyzing it (or speaking with plants) reveals it's a reaction to "screaming on the wind."
    
- **Harpers' Inquiry:** Gideon the toymaker approaches the party. He believes something is wrong in Mossfield and needs help investigating the fen before the town's sanity breaks. He can offer gold or future favors.
    
- **The Perfect Elixir:** Kaelen, frantic and sleepless, offers a large sum for a rare ingredient he claims is "the final component" for his masterpiece. _Lead:_ The ingredient must be gathered from the heart of the Dreamer's Fen, right where the Allip resides.
    
- **Corwyn's Hunch:** The old ranger will share his theory about an undead presence if the party buys him a drink and listens. He begs them to find the source and "put it to rest, for good."

#### **Quirk: The Shared Dream-Plague**

|d6|A Fragment of a Dream|
|---|---|

|3|"I see a man drowning in a pond, but he's not struggling. He's just... writing on a slate as he sinks."|
|4|"All my teeth are falling out and turning into little white pebbles. When I pick them up, they're covered in strange writing."|
|5|"I'm trying to scream a warning to everyone in the town square, but only bubbles come out of my mouth."|
|6|"I'm standing in a beautiful meadow, but the flowers are all made of glass, and they're shattering one by one from a sound I can't hear."|

> A picturesque town nestled between two roaring waterfalls, where the beauty of nature hides a culture of fierce, secretive hunters.

#### **Overview**

- **Population:** ~700
    
- **Vibe:** Proud, insular, and steeped in tradition. The air is filled with a constant, soothing roar from the twin waterfalls and the smell of clean water and pine. The people are hardy, self-sufficient, and deeply suspicious of outsiders. They value strength, tracking skill, and silence.
    
- **Theme:** Secrecy and Tradition. The town is built around a deep, old secret tied to the waterfalls and the forest. The hunters aren't just protecting game; they're protecting something much older and more powerful.
    
- **Secret:** The waterfalls are sacred to a tribe of **Reclusive Fey** known as the "River Sprites." The town's founding families made a pact: the sprites bless the waters and the hunt, and in return, the hunters protect their glade from discovery and ensure no metal or fire despoils the sacred area. The "suspicious" hunters are enforcing this ancient, forbidden pact.

#### **Adventure Hooks & Quests**

- **The Broken Pact:** Mara the elder confides in the party. The signs are there: the game is getting scarce, the water tastes flat, and the fey magic is fading. She believes the pact must be renewed or replaced, but Kaelen refuses to listen. She needs outside help to communicate with the sprites.
    
- **The Poacher's Offer:** Pike the trapper offers to lead the party to the "best hunting grounds" he's ever seen—if they help him stand up to the Twinbrook hunters who guard it.
    
- **The Missing Hunter:** A hunter failed to return from patrol near the falls. Huntmaster Kaelen tasks the party with finding him, warning them to stick to the path and touch nothing. _Lead:_ The hunter discovered Dovan's illegal logging and was attacked by a protective fey creature.
    
- **A Curious Artist:** Anya shows the party her sketches, pointing out the "pretty lights" she sees by the waterfall at dusk. She doesn't understand why her father told her to stop drawing them.
    
- **Strange Game:** Silas the butcher shows the party a recently killed deer with faintly glowing antlers and asks if they've ever seen anything like it. He's worried it's diseased or cursed.

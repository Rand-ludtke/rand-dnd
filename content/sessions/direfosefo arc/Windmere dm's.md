> A pleasant, wind-swept town that uses a local horror story as a source of identity, unaware that the story is starting to write a new chapter.

#### **Overview**

- **Population:** ~600
    
- **Vibe:** Cheerful, gossipy, and commercial. The town is known for its strong winds and stunning views. The people are friendly and entrepreneurial, having turned their local "haunt" into a tourist attraction. Underneath the cheerful exterior, a genuine unease is beginning to grow.
    
- **Theme:** Denial and Escalation. The town has lived alongside the story of the haunted manor for so long they've stopped believing it. Their commercialization of it has inadvertently attracted real supernatural attention, and the minor, explainable phenomena are becoming harder to ignore.
    
- **Secret:** **Windmere Manor is becoming actively haunted.** The playful dares and constant intrusion of visitors have disturbed a dormant presence within the house. A **Banshee** or **Poltergeist** is beginning to awaken, feeding on the fear and negative energy. The strange sounds and sights are no longer just the wind and imagination.

#### **Adventure Hooks & Quests**

- **The Historian's Concern:** Elara approaches the party. The town's jokes have gone too far, and she fears they are disrespecting the dead. She wants them to explore the manor _respectfully_ and document its true history to counter the silly stories.
    
- **The Ghost Hunter's Hire:** Gideon the fraud hires the party as "muscle" for a nighttime investigation of the manor. He plans to fake a phenomenon, but will get far more than he bargained for.
    
- **The Watch Captain's Request:** Borin can no longer ignore the reports. He asks the party, as neutral outsiders, to spend a night in the manor and find a rational explanation for the occurrences—or proof that it's all a prank.
    
- **A Dare Gone Wrong:** A group of teenagers (including Rook and Finn) went to the manor for a dare and haven't returned. Their parents are worried, and Mayor Evans is finally concerned.
    
- **The Shepherd's Warning:** Pike stops the party on the road into town. He points to the manor and says, "Something's woken up there. It's not a story anymore." He begs them to be careful.

## 🗺️ Travel Context

- The party leaves Direfosefo with suspicion about nearby towns.
    
- They plan to **check out Elensford and Redwick** to see if any Dragon Cult or other threats are lurking.
    
- Along the way: **minimal combat**, this session is focused on **roleplay, investigation, and narrative world-building**.

# Elensford (Chaotic Gossip Hell)

> _“Everyone’s got something to say, and none of it’s the same.”_

### Overview

- **Population:** ~180
    
- **Vibe:** Gossip-fueled chaos. Everyone talks fast, nobody agrees. A town where every barn has burned down _at least once_ and everyone has at least one enemy across the street.
    
- **Secret Underbelly:** There are **2 true facts hidden** in the madness:
    
    - The cook bears an **infernal symbol**.
        
    - A **devil flew overhead at night**.
        
- **Theme:** **Too much noise, buried signal**. Let players try to sort the mess.

# Town #2 – Redwick

> _"A pious town with too many empty beds."_

### Overview

- **Population:** ~160
    
- **Location:** 1 day northeast of Elensford.
    
- **Mood:** Devout, peaceful, and uneasy. Several families have gone quiet or “moved suddenly.”
    
- **Secret:** **People are vanishing**, likely taken by devils from Direfosefo as offerings or deals gone wrong.
    
- **Distraction Quest:** Strange phenomena at the graveyard (minor undead activity).
    

---

### 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Key NPCs

|Name|Role|Notes|
|---|---|---|
|**Brother Renald**|Head of Lathander temple|Worried about missing parishioners. Blames "lack of faith."|
|**Ilara Greenwell**|Local herbalist and gravekeeper|Notices disturbed graves and strange footprints. Very sharp.|
|**Mayor Teck**|Nervous administrator|Downplays the disappearances. Probably knows more.|
|**Gerra and Olvin Penn**|Farmers|Lost their adult son 3 weeks ago. Think he just ran off. He didn’t.|
|**Stetch the Undertaker**|Graveyard caretaker|Has seen something crawling out of an empty grave. Sleeps in a crypt now.|

---

### 🧩 Structure: Something Creeping

**What’s Going On:**

- At night, 1–2 corpses rise per week (ghouls, minor undead).
    
- It’s **not the main devil plot**, but rather **a side effect** of infernal corruption leaking from Direfosefo.
    
- One NPC may have made a desperate **bargain with a devil** to bring back a loved one, creating an infernal foothold.
    

---

### ⚔️ Optional Encounter

**Undead Interlude**

- **Ghouls or Shadows** attack at night, possibly in graveyard or outskirts.
    
- They bear faint sigils that match markings seen in Elensford.
    
- Combat lets martial characters shine, gives a **tangible threat**, and resets party tone.
    

---

### 🔮 Foreshadowing Clues

- Disappeared villagers were last seen speaking with **a traveling café supplier** (tie-in to Direfosefo devils).
    
- Some victims were found **with strange black coins**—made of iron, warm to the touch.
    
- One undead mutters, “The fire below tastes sweet…”
    

---

### 🎯 Session Goals in Redwick

- Let players get **distracted by a noble but irrelevant quest**.
    
- **Foreshadow** Direfosefo devil influence in subtle ways.
    
- Offer small combat (1–2 encounters max).
    
- Build suspense for their eventual return to Direfosefo.
    

---

## 🧠 GM Notes

- Lean into **humor and frustration** in Elensford: allow the party to _enjoy_ being caught in nonsense.
    
- Redwick should feel **darker and more unnerving**, showing the **shadow creeping out from Direfosefo**.
    
- NPCs in both towns may **mention strange café wagons** or **odd new trade routes**.
    
- Plant **1 devil name** across both towns that matches a sigil, rumor, or overheard word — something the party can follow up on later.

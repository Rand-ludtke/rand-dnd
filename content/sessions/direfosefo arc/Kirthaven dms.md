> A town of sawdust and suspicion, where the hum of industry masks a whispered heresy.

#### **Overview**

- **Population:** ~850 permanent residents, plus ~150 transient loggers and merchants.
    
- **Vibe:** A rough, practical, and industrious town. The air constantly smells of pine and fresh sawdust. The people are hardworking but wary of outsiders, protective of their trade secrets and community. There's an underlying tension between the wealthy business owners and the common laborers.
    
- **Theme:** Industry and Hidden Corruption. On the surface, it's all about logging and trade. Underneath, a dragon cult has infiltrated the town's leadership, using the lumber business as a cover for their activities.
    
- **Secret:** The **Cult of the Dragon** has a cell operating here, led by **Borin Ironwood** (the town's respected lumber baron). They are secretly harvesting a rare, magically-infused "Duskwood" from a hidden part of the forest, using it to build components for a dragon-themed ritual. They also smuggle cultists and supplies under the cover of regular lumber shipments.

#### **Adventure Hooks & Quests**

- **The Missing Crew:** Gareth's best logging crew failed to return from the western tract. He'll pay the party to find them. _Lead:_ They weren't lost; they stumbled upon the Duskwood grove and were captured by Marlen.
    
- **Bad Timber:** Captain Rennick has noticed a shipment of "special order" lumber being stored under heavy guard. It feels strangely cold to the touch. He wants to know what's really in those crates. _Lead:_ They contain ritually prepared Duskwood, destined for a dragon burial site.
    
- **The Whispering Woods:** Brother Donovan's visions are getting worse. He asks the party to accompany him to a sacred glade to perform a cleansing ritual. _Lead:_ The ritual is interrupted by cultists who don't want the forest's "corruption" cleansed.
    
- **A Silent Warning:** "Silent" Sam urgently tries to get the party's attention, then carves a complex image: a tree with a black dragon coiled around it, and an axe striking the tree.
    
- **Anya's Inquiry:** Borin's assistant, Anya, casually asks the party if they've "noticed the lack of certain birdsong" in the deeper woods—a clue something is magically amiss.

#### **Quirk: Town of Whispered Secrets**

The constant noise of the mills means people often have to speak closely to be heard, fostering a culture of whispers and secrets. When the party asks around or gathers information, roll on this table for a random rumor they might overhear.

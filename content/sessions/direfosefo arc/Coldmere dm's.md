> A lonely, quiet town where the ever-present lake mist muffles sound and hides secrets. The water is deep, and so are the town's troubles.

#### **Overview**

- **Population:** ~400
    
- **Vibe:** Isolated, somber, and eerie. The constant, cool mist from the lake blankets the town, deadening sounds and limiting visibility. The people are pale, quiet, and deeply superstitious. They speak in hushed tones and avoid the lake after dark.
    
- **Theme:** Grief and Hidden Depths. The town is in a state of quiet mourning, punctuated by fear of being the next to vanish. Their solution is a dark bargain they dare not speak of.
    
- **Secret:** The locals aren't just "vanishing." They are being taken as **tribute**. An ancient, dormant water spirit in the lake awoke hungry. The town's leader, **Elara**, made a pact with it: one person every full moon in exchange for the town's safety and bountiful fishing. The "vanishing" is the town silently selecting its outcasts and offering them to the lake.

#### **Adventure Hooks & Quests**

- **Find the Lost:** Kaelen, desperate for closure, will pay the party to investigate his son's disappearance. He begs them to dive down to the old ruins or find some other clue.
    
- **The Next Full Moon:** The party arrives a few days before the next offering is due. They can sense the town's dread mounting. Can they discover the secret and stop the ritual in time?
    
- **The Hunter's Tale:** Corwin the hunter asks for an escort to track a prized stag that fled _towards_ the lake—an unheard-of event. He's too scared to go alone.
    
- **The Spirit's Whim:** The water spirit, bored of its bland offerings, begins to demand something more specific: a magical item, a particular person, or for the party to retrieve a stolen trinket from its ruins.
    
- **A Priestess's Crisis:** Anya confides in a holy party member. Her prayers are going unanswered, and the oppressive evil is crushing her faith. She needs their strength and guidance to uncover the truth.

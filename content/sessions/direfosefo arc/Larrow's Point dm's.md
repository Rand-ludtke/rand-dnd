> A town perched on the edge of the world, where the wind sings through the cliffs and the company of eagles is valued more than gold.

#### **Overview**

- **Population:** ~550
    
- **Vibe:** Awe-inspiring, windswept, and proud. The air is crisp and thin. The people are hardy, independent, and have a deep respect for the natural world, especially the great eagles they bond with. They are cautious but respectful of those who prove themselves.
    
- **Theme:** Freedom and Tradition. The town is built around the ancient art of falconry, elevated to its highest form with the training of Giant Eagles. Their culture is defined by bonds of trust, patience, and mutual respect with these majestic creatures.
    
- **Secret:** The current **Eagle Lord, Kaelen**, is a fraud. He did not earn the trust of his eagle, **Suncrest**, through the traditional years of bonding. He used a forbidden charm spell from an old text to force the bond. The eagle is miserable and hostile, and the magic is slowly corrupting the natural order of the roost.

#### **Adventure Hooks & Quests**

- **A Bond Unraveling:** Rook or Elara approach the party. Suncrest's behavior is becoming dangerous. They need outsiders, unbiased by town politics, to help discover the cause. Is it a sickness? A curse?
    
- **The Lost Eaglet:** A young eaglet has gone missing from a lower roost. The Keepers are busy, and Finn is distraught. He begs the party to help him find it before the cliff cats do. _Lead:_ The eaglet isn't lost; it was scared away by Suncrest's unnatural rage.
    
- **A Test of Mettle:** Lord Kaelen, wanting to appear strong, challenges a party member to a test of skill—a race along a dangerous cliff path or an archery contest. He will cheat to win.
    
- **The Historian's Proof:** Corbin believes history is repeating itself. He asks the party to find physical proof of the past lord's failure—perhaps a journal in an old ruin or a specific relic.
    
- **The Translator's Concern:** Mira is troubled. She will confide in a well-spoken party member about the dark charm-spell text Kaelen had her translate, fearing what he has done.

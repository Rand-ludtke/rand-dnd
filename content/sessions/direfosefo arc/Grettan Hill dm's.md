> A sparse village living in the long shadow of history, where every dig unearths more than just dirt.

#### **Overview**

- **Population:** ~200
    
- **Vibe:** Remote, wary, and secretive. The wind whistles constantly over the hills. The people are a mix of poor shepherds and tight-lipped laborers who are clearly hiding something. A sense of anticipation and fear hangs in the air.
    
- **Theme:** Greed and Taboo. The village is defined by the presence of the ancient hillfort and the forbidden knowledge—and wealth—it promises. The desperate search for a better life is clashing with ancient warnings.
    
- **Secret:** Someone is indeed digging in the hillfort ruins, but it's not for treasure. A local shepherd, **Kaelen**, discovered a sealed oubliette containing the preserved, undead corpse of a **Necromancer** who was entombed alive after a failed uprising centuries ago. The necromancer has been whispering promises of power to Kaelen, guiding his digs to find its lost phylactery (a jeweled dagger) to complete its resurrection.

#### **Adventure Hooks & Quests**

- **The Wife's Plea:** Mara begs the party for help. Her husband Kaelen is sick, or cursed, or both. He leaves every night and comes back covered in dirt and muttering about "the master in the dark." She fears for his soul.
    
- **The Elder's Warning:** Elder Moira intercepts the party before they even reach the tavern. She tells them the hill is cursed and that they should leave for their own safety. If they stay, she begs them to find out who is digging and stop them.
    
- **A Shepherd's Concern:** Rook offers the party a reward to find out what's scaring his sheep. He blames the "fool diggers" and fears an ancient curse is seeping into the land.
    
- **The Tinker's Tale:** Gideon the tinker pulls the party aside. The rumors he's heard in other towns match what's happening here. He believes something truly evil is stirring and offers them a silver piece each to investigate the ruins.
    
- **The Boy's Secret:** Finn the shepherd boy brags to the party about knowing a "big secret" and offers to show them the "secret door" for a copper piece.

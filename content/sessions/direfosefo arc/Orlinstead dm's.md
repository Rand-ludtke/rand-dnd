> A simple, hardworking village where the soil is rich and trust is thin after a recent tragedy.

#### **Overview**

- **Population:** ~300
    
- **Vibe:** Agricultural, communal, and deeply paranoid. The air smells of loam, woodsmoke, and the lingering scent of char. The people are farmers and ranchers, normally friendly and open, but the recent fire has made them suspicious of everyone, especially outsiders.
    
- **Theme:** Fear and Scapegoating. The village is reeling from a devastating loss. The need to blame someone is overriding their common sense, making them easy to manipulate.
    
- **Secret:** The barn was **not** burned down by cultists. It was an accident caused by **Hobb**, the stablehand, who was sneaking a smoke break near a hay bale. Terrified of being blamed and exiled, he hid the evidence and was the first to shout "Cultists!" to divert suspicion. A charismatic traveling preacher, **Brother Merrick**, is fanning these fears for his own gain.

#### **Adventure Hooks & Quests**

- **Find the Truth:** Mayor Evans, desperate for a resolution, will privately hire the party to investigate the fire. He needs proof, one way or another, to calm the village.
    
- **A Preacher's Request:** Brother Merrick will "commission" the party to "hunt for signs of the cult" in the nearby woods. He hopes they will find (or plant) evidence to confirm his narrative and increase his influence.
    
- **The Guilty Man:** Anya the healer knows Hobb's guilt is killing him. She believes he will confess to someone impartial (the party) if approached with kindness rather than accusation.
    
- **Protection Racket:** Captain Valen wants to hire the party as "experienced consultants" to help train his militia and scout the area, playing on their fears to make a quick coin.
    
- **The Newcomer's Shadow:** If Pike's past is discovered (perhaps his tattoo is spotted), the mob will immediately turn on him. The party must either defend an innocent man or bring a guilty one to justice.

> A town renowned for its peaceful pleasures, now buzzing with anxious gossip after the disappearance of its favorite storyteller.

#### **Overview**

- **Population:** ~800
    
- **Vibe:** Idyllic, prosperous, and community-focused. The air is rich with the smell of fermenting hops and aging cheese. The people are generally cheerful, trusting, and proud of their town's famous exports. The bard's disappearance has introduced a note of sharp, unfamiliar anxiety.
    
- **Theme:** Comfort and Unease. The town is a haven of simple, good things. The disappearance of the bard is a stark contrast to this peace, suggesting either a dark underbelly or an external threat that has breached their walls.
    
- **Secret:** The bard, **Lyra**, didn't just disappear. She discovered that the town's beloved, award-winning **"Goldenwheel" cheese** is not made from a secret family recipe. The dairy's owner, **Kaelen**, is using a **banned alchemical additive** smuggled in from a disgraced wizard's tower to enhance the flavor and addictiveness. Lyra was going to expose him. Kaelen had her kidnapped and is holding her in a hidden cellar beneath his dairy, trying to decide what to do.

#### **Adventure Hooks & Quests**

- **The Apprentice's Plea:** Finn begs the party for help. The guards have given up, and the mayor doesn't seem to care. He has his master's notebook, which contains cryptic notes about "additives," "Gideon," and "golden lies."
    
- **The Brewer's Suspicion:** Rook from the brewery approaches the party. He thinks Kaelen is acting guilty and offers to pay them to investigate the dairy discreetly. He can't stand the thought of a townsman being involved.
    
- **The Merchant's Nerves:** Gideon the merchant is in town. If the party mentions Lyra's name or the dairy, he becomes visibly nervous and tries to leave quickly. He might pay them to forget they saw him.
    
- **A Historical Mystery:** Old Silas thinks the answer might lie in the past. He asks the party to find the old architectural plans for the dairy to see if there are any sealed-off rooms or forgotten cellars.
    
- **The Mayor's Offer:** Mayor Evans hires the party, not to find Lyra, but to prove she simply left town. He wants them to find evidence of a planned trip or a secret lover to quell the damaging rumors.

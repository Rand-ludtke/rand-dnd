### **Adventure Title: The Curse of the Sand Pharaoh**

#### **Adventure Hook**

The party receives a letter from **Velora Dinkwell**, an old friend and scholar, inviting them to visit her at an archaeological dig near the Great Sphinx. She mentions a mysterious discovery and hints at strange occurrences, including rumors of a curse. When the party arrives, they find themselves embroiled in a mystery involving a cursed tomb, treasure hunters, and a vengeful pharaoh’s legend.

---

### **Act 1: Arrival at the Sphinx**

The party arrives at the dig site, where they reunite with Velora. She introduces them to **Prince Omar**, the leader of the archaeological team, and explains they’ve uncovered an ancient necklace and a hidden entrance to the tomb of **Queen Nephretis**, a Cleopatra-like figure who cursed her treasure to protect it.

#### **Key NPCs:**

- **Velora Dinkwell**: A brilliant but slightly scatterbrained scholar (Velma-inspired).
    
- **Prince Omar**: A charming and knowledgeable archaeologist leading the restoration project.
    
- **Amahl Ali Akbar**: A friendly merchant and guide who offers to help the party navigate the desert.
    
- **Rock Rivers**: A disgraced adventurer and former host of _Fear Facers_, a show about uncovering supernatural mysteries. Rivers is desperate to prove himself after being caught faking evidence of curses and monsters.
    
- **Dr. Amelia Von Butch**: A ruthless treasure hunter who arrives with her team, determined to loot the tomb.
    

#### **The Sandstorm**

As the party explores the site, a sudden sandstorm forces everyone into the tomb. Dr. Von Butch’s team uses explosives to blast open the entrance, ignoring Velora’s warnings about the curse. The party must now navigate the tomb while uncovering the truth behind the curse.

---

### **Act 2: Investigating the Tomb**

The tomb is filled with traps, puzzles, and ancient hieroglyphs warning of the curse. The party must investigate the tomb, gather clues, and piece together the mystery while avoiding Amelia’s team, who are recklessly setting off traps and causing chaos.

#### **Key Clues:**

1. **The Petrified Prince**  
    The party finds Prince Omar seemingly turned to stone. Velora is distraught, but closer inspection reveals the "statue" is too perfect. (It’s actually a cleverly crafted decoy to scare off intruders.)
    
    - **Investigation Check (DC 12)**: The statue is made of plaster, not stone.
        
    - **Arcana Check (DC 15)**: There’s no magical residue, suggesting it’s not the result of a curse.
        
2. **The Hieroglyphs**  
    The walls are covered in hieroglyphs warning of the curse.
    
    - **History Check (DC 14)**: The hieroglyphs are unusually modern, suggesting they were added recently.
        
    - **Insight Check (DC 12)**: The warnings seem exaggerated, as if designed to scare people.
        
3. **The Undead Guardians**  
    The party encounters **animated statues** (use **Stone Cursed** or **Animated Armor** stats) that come to life to defend the tomb. These are not actual undead but magical constructs.
    
    - **Arcana Check (DC 13)**: The constructs are powered by simple enchantments, not necromancy.
        
4. **The Lost City Beneath the Tomb**  
    The party stumbles into a hidden underground city inhabited by descendants of Queen Nephretis’s servants. The city’s high priest, **Hotep**, mistakes one of the party members for the reincarnation of the pharaoh and insists on crowning them.
    
    - **Persuasion Check (DC 14)**: Convince Hotep to help them stop Amelia without being forced into a pharaoh’s duties.
        
    - **Investigation Check (DC 12)**: Hotep reveals that the "curse" was concocted by Prince Omar to protect the tomb.
        
5. **The Spirit of the Sand**  
    Hotep warns of the "Spirit of the Sand," a guardian that will awaken if the tomb is disturbed. The party encounters a **Sand Golem** (use **Earth Elemental** stats) that attacks them in a narrow chamber.
    
    - **Combat Encounter**: 1 Earth Elemental reflavored as a Sand Golem.
        
    - **Investigation Check (DC 15)**: The golem is a magical construct, not a supernatural being.
        

---

### **Act 3: Unmasking the Hoax**

The party reaches the main chamber, where Amelia is attempting to steal Queen Nephretis’s crown. As she does, the tomb begins to collapse, and the Nile bursts into the chamber, flooding it. The party must:

- Stop Amelia from escaping with the crown.
    
- Avoid being swept away by the rushing water.
    
- Rescue any NPCs (Velora, Prince Omar, etc.) who are in danger.
    

#### **The Big Reveal**

After subduing Amelia, the party discovers that the "curse" was a ruse orchestrated by Prince Omar and Velora to protect the tomb from treasure hunters. The "undead guardians" were actually Omar’s workers in disguise, and the "petrified" victims were statues. Amelia and her team are arrested, and the tomb is sealed for further study.

---

### **Epilogue**

The party is celebrated as heroes. Prince Omar thanks them for their help and offers them a share of the treasure (or a magical item from the tomb). Velora hints at another mystery she’d like their help with, setting up a potential sequel.

---

### **Treasure**

- **Necklace of the Sand Pharaoh**: A magical necklace that grants advantage on saving throws against petrification and once per day allows the wearer to cast _Meld into Stone_.
    
- **Crown of Queen Nephretis**: A ceremonial crown worth 1,000 gp, but cursed to attract the attention of greedy NPCs if worn.
    
- **Golden Scarab Amulet**: A magical amulet that grants +1 AC and allows the wearer to cast _Create or Destroy Water_ once per day.
    

---

### **Mystery Breakdown**

1. **The Curse**: The curse is a hoax designed to scare off treasure hunters. Clues include the plaster statue, modern hieroglyphs, and the lack of necromantic magic.
    
2. **The Guardians**: The "undead" are actually constructs or workers in disguise.
    
3. **The Spirit of the Sand**: A magical construct, not a supernatural being.
    
4. **The Flood**: The tomb’s collapse and flooding are natural consequences of Amelia’s reckless actions, not part of the curse.

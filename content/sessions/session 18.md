## 🧭 Session Prep – Journey Toward Direfosefo

### 🎯 Session Goals

- Present 1–2 quests that point toward Direfosefo.
    
- Introduce nearby towns (1–3 days travel).
    
- Start planting seeds of the **devil alliance** and the **dragon cult misdirection**.
    
- Let the party investigate, do quests, or explore the towns.
    

---

## 🗺️ Quest Hooks (Leading Toward Direfosefo)

> Use these at the session start to offer direction.

- **"Missing Merchant"** – A traveling merchant disappeared near the hills northeast of Goldfall. Last seen heading toward Direfosefo.
    
- **"Whispers in the Trees"** – Strange howling and missing livestock near one of the forest towns—locals say it’s the dragon cult, but something doesn’t add up.
    
- **"Courier Needed"** – A noble needs a letter delivered discreetly to someone in Direfosefo. (Could contain encoded information the devils want intercepted.)

## 🏙️ Direfosefo – Secrets and Lies

### Known:

- Childhood home of one or more PCs.
    
- Recently “prosperous” café (High Altitude Brews).
    
- Local devils helping some townsfolk prosper.
    

### Secret:

- New **mayor is a devil**.
    
- The **player's parents** made a pact with devils to grow their café empire.
    
- Devils want to **discredit and destroy the dragon cult** who are meddling in infernal plans.
    
- Players are being **manipulated into acting as pawns**.

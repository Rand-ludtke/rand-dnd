#### **Item Type:** Undead Pet

#### **Rarity:** Rare

#### **Attunement:** Yes

---

### **Description**

The **Cursed Hand** is a skeletal, undead appendage that fuses with the wearer’s own hand when equipped. Made of ancient bone and blackened by necrotic magic, it retains a mind of its own—occasionally moving independently to assist or even hinder its host. Despite its eerie nature, many rogue-like adventurers seek it for its **enhanced dexterity and supernatural reflexes**.

---

### **Effects**

 **Dexterous Aid:** The skeletal hand grants **+2 to Dexterity checks** (Sleight of Hand, Acrobatics, etc.), as it sometimes takes over to guide precise movements.

 **Unnatural Reflexes:** Once per short rest, the hand **automatically catches** a thrown object or weapon (if it weighs under 5 lbs) without requiring a check.

 **Phantom Grip:** The hand can move slightly **independently**—writing, picking locks, or even making rude gestures on its own.

---

### **Curses & Quirks**

 **Uncontrollable Moments:** The hand occasionally **moves on its own**, performing **minor actions** that the wearer doesn’t intend (e.g., knocking over a cup, flipping a coin unexpectedly, or waving at strangers).

 **Gravebound:** The hand is **detectable as undead**, meaning divine magic like _Detect Evil and Good_ or _Turn Undead_ may expose it.

**Restless Grip:** If unattuned for more than 24 hours, the hand **attempts to crawl away**, seeking a new host

### **Passive Ability Options**

1. **Petrifying Blood** – Gain resistance to poison damage. Additionally, once per long rest, you can reroll a failed saving throw against being petrified or poisoned.
2. **Vital Bloom** – Your blood pulses with the energy of the Bloodbloom. When you roll a Hit Die to regain HP during a short rest, add an extra 1d6 to the healing.
3. **Death's Glare** – Learn the _Ray of Enfeeblement_ spell and can cast it once per long rest without using a spell slot.
4. **Lingering Vitality** – If you drop to 0 HP but are not outright killed, you stay conscious until the end of your next turn before falling unconscious.
5. **Serpentine Guile** – Gain proficiency in Deception or Persuasion (your choice). If you already have proficiency, you add double your proficiency bonus to checks made with that skill.

### **Active Ability Options**

1. **Basilisk’s Stare** _(Action, Recharge 5-6)_ – You force a creature within 30 feet to make a DC 14 Constitution saving throw. On a failure, their flesh stiffens, reducing their movement speed to 0 and imposing disadvantage on Dexterity saves until the end of their next turn.
2. **Sanguine Bloom** _(Bonus Action, 1/Long Rest)_ – You can sacrifice 10 HP to gain advantage on all attack rolls, saving throws, and ability checks until the start of your next turn.
3. **Blood to Stone** _(Action, 1/Long Rest)_ – A target within 10 feet must make a DC 14 Constitution save. On a failure, they are restrained as their blood thickens into stone-like veins for 1 minute. They can repeat the save at the end of each of their turns.
4. **Venomous Rebirth** _(Reaction, 1/Long Rest)_ – When reduced to 0 HP but not killed outright, you instead drop to 1 HP and immediately exude a toxic aura. Each creature within 10 feet must make a DC 14 Constitution saving throw or take 3d6 poison damage.
5. **Thorned Vitae** _(Bonus Action, 2/Short Rest)_ – You infuse your next melee or spell attack with the essence of the Bloodbloom. On a hit, the target takes an additional 2d6 necrotic damage, and you regain HP equal to half the necrotic damage dealt.

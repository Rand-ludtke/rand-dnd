### Notes:

- **Days Remaining:** Roll **1d4** whenever a new material is added to determine processing time.
    
- **Abilities:** Roll **1d20** after processing is complete to determine the specific potion effect.
    
- **Player Reminder:** Only one magical material can be processed at a time in the jar.

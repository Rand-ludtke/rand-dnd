**Location:** Crafting Quarter, Major Port Trading Hub  
**Type:** Tailor & Clothier

A prestigious tailor shop catering to merchants, sailors, and nobles alike, _The Silk & Anchor_ offers both practical and elegant attire. Known for its high-quality craftsmanship, the shop also stocks a selection of magical garments for discerning customers. The shop's emblem—a golden anchor entwined with silver thread—symbolizes reliability and refinement.

## **NPCs**

### **Master Tailor Lysara Valenn** (Half-Elf)(in love with sarro)

- A renowned tailor with a sharp eye for detail and a preference for elegant designs.
- Formerly worked in noble courts but moved to the port for greater trade opportunities.
- Speaks multiple languages and has connections to exotic textile merchants.

### **Bran "Quickstitch" Tallow** (Halfling)

- The shop’s expert in practical wear, specializing in durable sailor’s gear and travel-ready attire.
- Friendly and chatty, he loves sharing gossip with customers.
- Often found adjusting garments at lightning speed with enchanted needles.

### **Kaela Ironweave** (Dwarf)

- A specialist in armor-weave clothing and reinforced outfits.
- Prefers function over fashion but creates some of the most durable attire in town.
- Works with blacksmiths to blend fine metal threading into luxurious fabrics.

## **Atmosphere & Design**

The shop is decorated with bolts of rich fabric, mannequins displaying the latest trends, and a set of enchanted needles that stitch without hands. A large window showcases elegant outfits, while the scent of fresh linen and candle wax fills the air.

**
#### **Overview**

- **Population:** ~400 (and shrinking)
    
- **Vibe:** Grim, desperate, and weary. The air tastes of dust and despair. The people are hardened by labor and loss, their hopes worn thin. They are survivors, but their will is breaking under a new, unseen pressure.


---

#### **Notable NPCs**

| Name            | Role                     | Personality / Gimmick                                                                                |
| --------------- | ------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Elder Moira** | Town Leader              | A grim, tired woman who has buried too many friends. Holds the town together by sheer force of will. |
| **Kaelen**      | Owner of The Dusty Helm  | The town's only publican. Serves bitter ale and thin stew. A realist with little hope left.          |
| **Borin**       | The Last Miner           | Grizzled, missing two fingers, and haunted by what he's seen.                                        |
| **Mara**        | The Healer               | Exhausted and overwhelmed. Treats wounds, coughs, and a strange new malady: a deep, waking lethargy. |
| **Rook**        | Head of the Watch (of 2) | Cynical and defeated. His "patrols" are a circuit of the town's edge.                                |
| **Finn**        | Kaelen's Grandson        | A quiet, serious boy who has known only hardship.                                                    |
| **Thorne**      | The Blacksmith           | His forge is cold more often than not. Repairs tools, rarely makes new ones.                         |
| **Elara**       | A Widow                  | Lost her husband to a mine collapse years ago. Now takes in mending.                                 |
| **Gideon**      | A Scavenger              | Brave, foolish, or desperate enough to sneak into the mine's upper levels for scrap metal.           |
| **Pike**        | A Trapper                | Operates on the edge of town. Gaunt and jumpy.                                                       |
| **Anya**        | The Teacher              | Runs a makeshift school for the town's few children. Tries to maintain normality.                    |
| **Corwin**      | The Gravedigger          | The busiest man in town. Morbidly pragmatic.                                                         |
| **Silas**       | The Hermit               | Lives in a shack on the outskirts. Avoids people.                                                    |
| **Liana**       | Kaelen's Daughter        | Works at the inn. Tries to keep her father and son (Finn) from succumbing to despair.                |
| **Dovan**       | A Laborer                | Strong and simple. Does any work he can find.                                                        |

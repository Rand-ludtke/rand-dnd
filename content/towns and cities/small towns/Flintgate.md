**
#### **Overview**

- **Population:** ~400 (and shrinking)
    
- **Vibe:** Grim, desperate, and weary. The air tastes of dust and despair. The people are hardened by labor and loss, their hopes worn thin. They are survivors, but their will is breaking under a new, unseen pressure.


---

#### **Notable NPCs**

|Name|Role|Personality / Gimmick|Secret/Quirk|
|---|---|---|---|
|**Elder Moira**|Town Leader|A grim, tired woman who has buried too many friends. Holds the town together by sheer force of will.|Knows the mine's evil is intelligent and targeted. She fears it's a curse they cannot break.|
|**Kaelen**|Owner of The Dusty Helm|The town's only publican. Serves bitter ale and thin stew. A realist with little hope left.|His son was the last miner to disappear. He blames himself for letting him go into the mine.|
|**Borin**|The Last Miner|Grizzled, missing two fingers, and haunted by what he's seen.|He was part of the crew that broke into the old tunnel where they "woke something up." He never went back.|
|**Mara**|The Healer|Exhausted and overwhelmed. Treats wounds, coughs, and a strange new malady: a deep, waking lethargy.|The sickness isn't physical. It's the hags' magic, draining the victims' will to live.|
|**Rook**|Head of the Watch (of 2)|Cynical and defeated. His "patrols" are a circuit of the town's edge.|Found strange, three-toed tracks circling the town on a foggy morning. Not human, not animal.|
|**Finn**|Kaelen's Grandson|A quiet, serious boy who has known only hardship.|Has started sleepwalking towards the mine entrance. He draws pictures of "three old ladies in the dark."|
|**Thorne**|The Blacksmith|His forge is cold more often than not. Repairs tools, rarely makes new ones.|Was hired to reinforce the barricade on the mine entrance. He says the metal was ice-cold and hummed with a foul energy.|
|**Elara**|A Widow|Lost her husband to a mine collapse years ago. Now takes in mending.|Her nightmares are so vivid she's afraid to sleep. In them, three crones offer her a way to see her husband again.|
|**Gideon**|A Scavenger|Brave, foolish, or desperate enough to sneak into the mine's upper levels for scrap metal.|Claims he saw faint, witch-fire lights deep in the main shaft and heard faint, rhythmic chanting.|
|**Pike**|A Trapper|Operates on the edge of town. Gaunt and jumpy.|His traps are always empty or contain animals that have rotted away in hours. The game is gone or tainted.|
|**Anya**|The Teacher|Runs a makeshift school for the town's few children. Tries to maintain normality.|The children's games have become dark, revolving around "getting caught by the mine ladies."|
|**Corwin**|The Gravedigger|The busiest man in town. Morbidly pragmatic.|The soil in the cemetery has become sour and black, and he swears the graves look... disturbed... some mornings.|
|**Silas**|The Hermit|Lives in a shack on the outskirts. Avoids people.|Came to Flintgate to study the mine's unique geology. Believes the hags are attracted to a latent psychic mineral.|
|**Liana**|Kaelen's Daughter|Works at the inn. Tries to keep her father and son (Finn) from succumbing to despair.|Is secretly saving every copper to buy a way out of town for her and Finn. She's almost there.|
|**Dovan**|A Laborer|Strong and simple. Does any work he can find.|Was offered a week's wages to carry a heavy, reeking sack into the mine for "a stranger." He refused.|

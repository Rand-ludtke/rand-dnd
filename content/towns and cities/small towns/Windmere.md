
#### **Overview**

- **Population:** ~600
    
- **Vibe:** Cheerful, gossipy, and commercial. The town is known for its strong winds and stunning views. The people are friendly and entrepreneurial, having turned their local "haunt" into a tourist attraction. Underneath the cheerful exterior, a genuine unease is beginning to grow.

#### **Locations & Shops**

|Building Name|Owner|Description|
|---|---|---|
|**The Howling Wolf Inn**|Kaelen|The center of the town's ghost tourism. Decorated with fake cobwebs and "cursed" artifacts.|
|**Windmere Manor**|_Abandoned_|A large, decaying manor house on a hill overlooking the town. Boards cover its windows, and the wind whistles through its architecture.|
|**The Specter's Snack**|Mara|A cozy stall on the town green selling themed treats like "ectoplasm cider" and "grave dirt brownies."|
|**The History House**|Elara|A small, meticulously kept museum with the real, sobering history of the manor's former family. Rarely visited.|
|**The Watch Post**|Borin|A small building where the town's two part-time guards base their operations.|
|**The Winding Path**|_The Town_|The well-trodden path from the town green up the hill to the manor. Lined with vendor stalls on festival days.|
|**The Overlook**|_The Town_|A scenic viewpoint that offers the best (and safest) view of the manor from a distance.|
|**Corwin's Gallery**|Corwin|A small studio where the artist sells his paintings. The newer ones have a disturbing quality.|

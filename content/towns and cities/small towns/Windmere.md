
#### **Overview**

- **Population:** ~600
    
- **Vibe:** Cheerful, gossipy, and commercial. The town is known for its strong winds and stunning views. The people are friendly and entrepreneurial, having turned their local "haunt" into a tourist attraction. Underneath the cheerful exterior, a genuine unease is beginning to grow.

---

#### **Notable NPCs**

|Name|Role|Personality / Gimmick|Secret/Quirk|
|---|---|---|---|
|**Mayor Evans**|Town Leader|A jovial salesman at heart. Sees the manor as the town's greatest asset.|Dismisses all new reports as "good for business." He is willfully ignorant of the growing danger.|
|**Kaelen**|Owner of The Howling Wolf|Boastful, loves a tall tale. His inn is the headquarters for dares to the manor.|Offers a free stay to anyone who brings back "proof" of a ghost (a specific trinket from inside).|
|**Elara**|Local Historian|Prim, proper, and frustrated by the town's disregard for facts.|Has the manor's actual, tragic history documented. Knows the stories are wrong, and the truth is far sadder.|
|**Borin**|Head of the Town Watch|A practical man with little patience for ghost stories.|Is secretly concerned. The recent "pranks" (objects moving, laughter) show a intelligence he can't explain.|
|**Mara**|Owner of The Specter's Snack|Sells "ghost-themed" pastries and hot cider to tourists.|Doesn't believe in ghosts, but is a superstitious woman. She's started wearing a protective charm.|
|**Rook**|A Teenager|The ringleader of the local youths. Bold and reckless.|Was the first to hear the distinct sound of weeping instead of just wind during a dare. He's too scared to admit it.|
|**Finn**|Rook's Friend|A follower, not a leader. Easily scared.|Claims a window in the manor slammed shut on its own, nearly catching his fingers. Nobody believes him.|
|**Thorne**|Retired Carpenter|Old, grouchy, and built half the town.|His father helped board up the manor decades ago. He says they did it to "keep something in, not out."|
|**Gideon**|Traveling Ghost Hunter|Dressed in all black, covered in dubious magical gadgets.|A complete fraud, but he's arrived because he's sensed the same shift in energy that a real practitioner would.|
|**Pike**|A Shepherd|Works the hills around the manor. Grounded and sensible.|His dog refuses to go within 100 yards of the manor now, whining and pulling at its leash.|
|**Anya**|Kaelen's Daughter|Helps run the inn. Romantic and imaginative.|Writes dramatic stories about the "lonely manor ghost" and leaves them for guests to find.|
|**Corwin**|Local Artist|Paints moody landscapes of the manor for tourists.|His latest paintings, done from life, have faint, pale faces in the upper windows that he didn't paint.|
|**Silas**|The Blacksmith|A no-nonsense man who makes tools and horseshoes.|Was hired to replace the manor's old iron gate. The old iron was cold to the touch and covered in rust that looked like dried blood.|
|**Liana**|Town Gossip|Knows everything about everyone and loves to share.|Is the source of most of the exaggerated rumors, but now the rumors she's hearing are starting to scare even her.|
|**Dovan**|A Gardener|Maintains the town's gardens and the overgrown path to the manor.|The plants near the manor are wilted and blackened, as if touched by frost, despite the warm weather.|

#### **Locations & Shops**

|Building Name|Owner|Description|
|---|---|---|
|**The Howling Wolf Inn**|Kaelen|The center of the town's ghost tourism. Decorated with fake cobwebs and "cursed" artifacts.|
|**Windmere Manor**|_Abandoned_|A large, decaying manor house on a hill overlooking the town. Boards cover its windows, and the wind whistles through its architecture.|
|**The Specter's Snack**|Mara|A cozy stall on the town green selling themed treats like "ectoplasm cider" and "grave dirt brownies."|
|**The History House**|Elara|A small, meticulously kept museum with the real, sobering history of the manor's former family. Rarely visited.|
|**The Watch Post**|Borin|A small building where the town's two part-time guards base their operations.|
|**The Winding Path**|_The Town_|The well-trodden path from the town green up the hill to the manor. Lined with vendor stalls on festival days.|
|**The Overlook**|_The Town_|A scenic viewpoint that offers the best (and safest) view of the manor from a distance.|
|**Corwin's Gallery**|Corwin|A small studio where the artist sells his paintings. The newer ones have a disturbing quality.|


#### **Overview**

- **Population:** ~700
    
- **Vibe:** Proud, insular, and steeped in tradition. The air is filled with a constant, soothing roar from the twin waterfalls and the smell of clean water and pine. The people are hardy, self-sufficient, and deeply suspicious of outsiders. They value strength, tracking skill, and silence.

---

#### **Notable NPCs**

|Name|Role|Personality / Gimmick|Secret/Quirk|
|---|---|---|---|
|**Kaelen**|Huntmaster|Grim, imposing, with an aura of absolute authority. His word is law among the hunters.|The current keeper of the fey pact. He will do anything, including kill, to protect the secret.|
|**Elara**|Kaelen's Daughter|A skilled hunter in her own right, but more curious and open-minded than her father.|She fears the pact is becoming a chain that holds the town back and secretly yearns for contact with the outside world.|
|**Borin**|Owner of The Falling Timber|Jovial and loud, but it feels forced. His cheer is a mask for the town's underlying tension.|Serves as Kaelen's eyes and ears in the tavern, reporting on any outsiders who ask too many questions.|
|**Mara**|Town Elder & Herbalist|The oldest resident, her knowledge of the old ways is unparalleled. Speaks to the plants.|Was the pact-keeper before Kaelen. She believes the fey's power is waning and the pact is failing.|
|**Rook**|Kaelen's Lieutenant|Fanatically loyal to Kaelen and the old ways. More zealous and cruel than his master.|Enjoys the power the pact gives him to intimidate others. He actively hunts for breaches of the rules.|
|**Finn**|Young Aspiring Hunter|Eager to prove himself and earn his place in the hunter's lodge.|Unknowingly broke the pact by taking a metal trap into the sacred grove. He's terrified of being found out.|
|**Thorne**|Retired Hunter|Lost his leg to a owlbear. Now carves intricate animal figurines.|Regrets the things he did to uphold the pact in his youth. Might warn a sympathetic party away.|
|**Gideon**|The Miller|Runs the water mill. Practical and focused on his work, avoids town politics.|His mill is powered by the sacred water. He's noticed the water levels fluctuating strangely lately.|
|**Lyra**|Weaver|Weaves beautiful patterns using dyes made from local plants and minerals.|Her patterns are based on old fey designs she finds on stones in the river. She doesn't know their meaning.|
|**Pike**|Trapper (Outsider)|Doesn't live in town, but operates on the very edge of their territory. Grizzled and independent.|Hates the Twinbrook hunters for running him off good trapping ground. Holds a grudge and knows they're hiding something.|
|**Anya**|Gideon's Daughter|Dreamy and artistic, often sketches the waterfalls and forests.|Her sketches accidentally include glimpses of the fey, though she draws them as "strange lights."|
|**Corwin**|Local Guide|Knows the safe paths and good fishing spots for miles around.|Strictly forbidden from taking clients near the twin waterfalls. He claims it's due to "dangerous currents."|
|**Silas**|The Butcher|Bloodstained apron, methodical and quiet. Processes the hunters' kills.|Has noticed the game brought in from the deep woods sometimes has... unusual properties. Glowing eyes, strangely shaped organs.|
|**Kella**|Owner of The Fisher's Hook|Runs a small bait and tackle shop. Knows everything about the river fish.|The fish near the waterfalls are larger and smarter, almost uncatchable. She thinks it's magic.|
|**Dovan**|Woodcarver|A rival to Thorne, but focuses on practical items like bowls and cups.|Uses wood sourced from the sacred grove (a major breach of the pact) because it's so much easier to carve and holds a beautiful shine.|

#### **Locations & Shops**

|Building Name|Owner|Description|
|---|---|---|
|**The Falling Timber**|Borin|The town's only inn and tavern. Rustic, filled with trophies from the hunt. The food is hearty and game-based.|
|**The Hunter's Lodge**|Kaelen|A large, off-limits longhouse where the hunters gather. Outsiders are not welcome.|
|**Gideon's Mill**|Gideon|A large watermill that provides flour for the town and surrounding area. The constant sound is soothing.|
|**The Twin Falls**|_The Fey_|The breathtaking waterfalls that give the town its name. The pool below is crystal clear and icy cold. The sacred grove is hidden behind one of them.|
|**Mara's Garden**|Mara|A beautifully kept herb garden behind the elder's house, featuring many rare and magical plants.|
|**The Butcher's Block**|Silas|A clean, efficient, but unmistakably bloody shop where game is processed and sold.|
|**The Fisher's Hook**|Kella|A small shack by the river selling fishing poles, nets, and bait.|
|**The Woodcarver's Hut**|Dovan|A workshop filled with the smell of fresh wood. Shavings cover the floor.|


#### **Overview**

- **Population:** ~1,200 (highly transient)
    
- **Vibe:** Busy, loud, and mercantile. The air smells of dust, sweat, and cooking food from a dozen different cultures. The town is a melting pot of traders, mercenaries, pilgrims, and scoundrels, all passing through. Trust is scarce, but information is plentiful—for a price.

#### **Locations & Shops**

|Building Name|Owner|Description|
|---|---|---|
|**The Wayfarer's Respite**|Kaelen|The famous tavern. A massive, multi-story building that is always packed. The heart of the town's information trade.|
|**The Dusty Wheel**|Dovan|The second, less popular inn. cheaper, dirtier, and patronized by those avoiding attention.|
|**Mara's Marvels**|Mara|A colorful wagon turned shopfront, filled with glowing baubles and exotic smells.|
|**The Curious Ocelot**|Lyra|A bookshop that also serves as a scribe and translator service. Dusty and quiet.|
|**Thora's Forge**|Thora|A loud, hot, but reliable smithy. The sign of a gauntlet holding a lightning bolt hints at her past.|
|**The Grand Bazaar**|_Various_|A permanent open-air market filling the town square. You can find any common good here, and many uncommon ones.|
|**Silas's Counting House**|Silas|A well-guarded, nondescript building where loans are made and debts are collected.|
|**Gideon's Stables**|Gideon|The largest stable complex for miles. Can accommodate dozens of wagons and hundreds of horses.|


> A peaceful, somber town built upon history's echo, where the people honor the past so thoroughly that they sometimes fail to look to the future.

#### **Overview**

- **Population:** ~500
    
- **Vibe:** Peaceful, respectful, and slightly melancholic. The air is clean and still. The people are calm, polite, and deeply traditional. They are farmers, artisans, and historians, not warriors. The town's morbid name is spoken with pride, not fear; it is a reminder of the cost of peace.

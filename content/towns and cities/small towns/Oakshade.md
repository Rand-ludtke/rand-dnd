
#### **Overview**

- **Population:** ~250
    
- **Vibe:** Sleepy, secretive, and slightly dishonest. The air is still and heavy under the canopy of large oak trees. The people are tight-knit, wary of outsiders, and comfortable with cutting corners. They have a collective secret they protect.
 
---

#### **Notable NPCs**

|Name|Role|Personality / Gimmick|Secret/Quirk|
|---|---|---|---|
|**Elder Moira**|Village Leader|Sharp-eyed and pragmatic. Tolerates small crimes but has a line she won't cross.|Is deeply suspicious of Kaelen. She knows a real dragonbone relic would be worth a fortune and attract dangerous attention.|
|**Kaelen**|The Shady Merchant|Charismatic, smarmy, and always looking for a deal. Dresses in flamboyant clothes.|**Secret:** His entire operation is a scam powered by a real, corrupting dragon tooth he doesn't know how to control.|
|**Borin**|Owner of The Leaky Barrel|The village publican. Knows everything and says little. Serves decent ale.|Acts as a fence for the village's poached game and pilfered goods. Sees Kaelen as unprofessional competition.|
|**Mara**|The Poacher|A skilled hunter and trapper who operates outside the law. Quiet and dangerous.|Is jealous of Kaelen's success. She's been following him, trying to discover his "supply" route to steal it.|
|**Rook**|Local Woodcutter|Strong, simple, and honest. Doesn't like the changes Kaelen has brought.|His favorite hunting dog got sick after chewing on a "dragonbone" knucklebone Kaelen sold him.|
|**Finn**|Village Idler|Lazy but clever. Hangs around Kaelen, hoping for easy coin.|Runs errands for Kaelen. He's seen the merchant talking to a large, scary-looking tooth in a locked box.|
|**Thorne**|The Crooked Cartographer|Sells maps to hidden trails and poaching grounds. Untrustworthy.|Sold Kaelen a map to a "dragon's burial site" that he completely made up. He's terrified Kaelen actually found something there.|
|**Elara**|Kaelen's Mark (Farmer)|A kind, gullible farmer who believes in stories.|Spent her life savings on a "dragonbone charm" to protect her sickly sheep. It isn't working.|
|**Gideon**|Traveling Priest|A kind-hearted cleric of a nature god. Passes through occasionally.|Senses a "foul magic" and a "grieving spirit" clinging to Kaelen's wagon. He doesn't understand the source.|
|**Pike**|A Forrester|Officially patrols the woods for the local lord. Unofficially looks the other way for a price.|Has been paid by Kaelen to forget he saw him digging in a restricted part of the forest.|
|**Anya**|Borin's Daughter|Sharp-witted and observant. Helps run the tavern.|Noticed that the few travelers who buy from Kaelen seem to have unusually bad luck on the road afterwards.|
|**Corwin**|A Hunter|Moral and upright. Disapproves of the village's illicit activities.|Is gathering evidence to report the village's poaching to the local lord. Kaelen is now on his list.|
|**Silas**|The Old Trapper|Lives deep in the woods. Rarely comes to town.|The animals are acting strangely near Kaelen's dig site—aggressive and sickly. He thinks the merchant poisoned them.|
|**Liana**|A Weaver|Makes beautiful baskets and cloth.|Wove a special padded case for Kaelen's "most precious artifact." It felt unnaturally cold and heavy.|
|**Dovan**|The Blacksmith|A decent man trying to make an honest living.|Was asked by Kaelen to craft a special stand for a "large fang." The metal of his tools grew brittle and cold during the work.|

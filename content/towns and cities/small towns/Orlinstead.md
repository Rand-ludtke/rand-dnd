
> A simple, hardworking village where the soil is rich and trust is thin after a recent tragedy.

#### **Overview**

- **Population:** ~300
    
- **Vibe:** Agricultural, communal, and deeply paranoid. The air smells of loam, woodsmoke, and the lingering scent of char. The people are farmers and ranchers, normally friendly and open, but the recent fire has made them suspicious of everyone, especially outsiders.

---

#### **Notable NPCs**

|Name|Role|Personality / Gimmick|Secret/Quirk|
|---|---|---|---|
|**Mayor Evans**|Village Leader|A pragmatic farmer chosen for his level head, but currently out of his depth.|Privately doubts the cultist story but feels pressured by the mob to agree. Desperate for any answer.|
|**Hobb**|Stablehand for the burned barn|Twitchy, nervous, and avoids eye contact. Jumps at loud noises.|**Secret:** He accidentally started the fire and is consumed by guilt and fear.|
|**Brother Merrick**|Traveling Preacher|Zealous, charismatic, and speaks in absolutes. Sees signs of evil in every shadow.|**Secret:** A fraud. He uses fear to gain followers and donations. He enjoys the power it gives him.|
|**Mara**|Owner of The Harvest Home|Stern, no-nonsense, and the village's unofficial matriarch. Her word carries weight.|Knows Hobb is hiding something but thinks he just saw the real culprits and is scared to talk.|
|**Old Thom**|The Old Shepherd|Grumpy, cynical, and has seen many village panics come and go.|Openly scoffs at the "cultist" story. Believes it was lightning or simple carelessness.|
|**Liam**|Blacksmith|Strong, quiet, and thoughtful. His forge was used to make new hardware for the rebuild.|Lost a significant amount of valuable tools and custom orders in the fire. Has a real, tangible reason to be angry.|
|**Elara**|Young Farmhand|Idealistic, brave, and hates the atmosphere of suspicion.|Is organizing a night watch among the younger villagers to "protect" the town, potentially starting a witch hunt.|
|**Jasper**|Tiller's Son|The village gossip. Knows everyone's business and is happy to share it.|Claims he saw "hooded figures" near the millpond the night of the fire (he saw raccoons).|
|**Captain Valen**|Militia Captain (of 2)|Inflated sense of importance. Now carries a spear everywhere.|Is using the crisis to push for a larger militia and a stockade wall, things the village can't afford.|
|**Anya**|Village Healer/Midwife|Calm, gentle, and practical. Treats burns and anxiety in equal measure.|Is treating Hobb for "nerves" and knows his guilt is making him physically ill.|
|**Bram**|The Victim (Farm Owner)|Heartbroken, angry, and ruined. Lost his livelihood, his livestock, and his winter feed.|Wants vengeance more than answers. Will latch onto any theory that gives him a target for his rage.|
|**Pike**|Traveling Laborer|Just arrived looking for work. Strong and willing.|Has a faded Dragon Cult tattoo hidden under his bracer, from a past he desperately wants to leave behind.|
|**Cora**|Bram's Daughter|Determined and resilient. Focused on rebuilding, not blaming.|Secretly relieved the barn is gone, as her father was drowning in debt. Sees a chance for a fresh start.|
|**Finn**|Village Idler|Lazy but good-natured. Avoids work if he can.|Was supposed to be watching the barn that night but was off flirting. His silence helps fuel Hobb's lie.|
|**Kella**|Weaver|Creates practical, durable work clothes.|Is doing a booming business replacing the clothes and sacks lost in the fire. Profiting from the tragedy.|


#### **Overview**

- **Population:** ~600
    
- **Vibe:** Peaceful, secluded, and slightly eerie. The air is thick with the smell of damp earth and a hundred different herbs. The townsfolk are friendly but reserved, with a deep, almost spiritual connection to the surrounding marsh and forest. They speak softly and move with purpose.

#### **Locations & Shops**

|Building Name|Owner|Description|
|---|---|---|
|**The Restful Meadow**|Hester|The town's cozy inn and tavern. Known for its hearty vegetable stew and incredibly comfortable, herb-stuffed mattresses.|
|**Moira's Mercy**|Elder Moira|The main herbalist shop. A cluttered, fragrant hut filled with drying herbs, jars of salves, and potions of all colors.|
|**Kaelen's Concoctions**|Kaelen|A modern alchemy lab set up in a refurbished shed. smells of chemicals and ozone. Features intricate glassware.|
|**The Looming Truth**|Liana|The weaver's workshop, filled with colorful threads and half-finished tapestries. The newer ones are dark and disturbing.|
|**The Mossy Tomb**|_Gravedigger_|The town's cemetery. The graves are shallow due to the water table, and some look recently disturbed (by natural sinkage).|
|**The Stillwater Library**|Petra|A small, damp building housing the town's records and a few precious books. Meticulously organized.|
|**Bram's Guided Tours**|Bram|Not a building, but a small signpost by the edge of town offering "Safe Passage" into the marsh for a fee.|
|**The Gnarled Branch**|Corwyn|A workshop attached to Corwyn's home, filled with half-carved wood and the smell of sawdust. He sells excellent walking sticks.|

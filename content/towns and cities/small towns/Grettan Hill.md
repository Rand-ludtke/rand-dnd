
#### **Overview**

- **Population:** ~200
    
- **Vibe:** Remote, wary, and secretive. The wind whistles constantly over the hills. The people are a mix of poor shepherds and tight-lipped laborers who are clearly hiding something. A sense of anticipation and fear hangs in the air.

---

#### **Locations & Shops**

|Building Name|Owner|Description|
|---|---|---|
|**The Shepherd's Rest**|Borin|The village's only inn and tavern. A dim, smoky room that serves as the central hub.|
|**Grettan Hillfort**|_Abandoned_|The ancient, crumbling stone ruins atop the largest hill. The site of the forbidden digging.|
|**The Common Pasture**|_The Village_|The shared grazing land for the village's sheep. The animals are skittish.|
|**Thorne's Hovel**|Thorne|A small shed with a forge attached. The glow of the fire is visible at night.|
|**The Old Well**|_The Village_|The village's water source. The water has had a faint, earthy taste lately.|
|**Kaelen's Cottage**|Kaelen|A small, simple shepherd's hut on the edge of the village. The garden is neglected.|
|**The Burial Mound**|_The Ancients_|A much older, grassy burial mound near the hillfort. Strictly forbidden ground according to village lore.|

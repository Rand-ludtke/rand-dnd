> A town of sawdust and suspicion, where the hum of industry masks a whispered heresy.

#### **Overview**

- **Population:** ~850 permanent residents, plus ~150 transient loggers and merchants.

---

#### **Notable NPCs**

|Name|Role|Personality / Gimmick|Secret/Quirk|
|---|---|---|---|
|**Borin Ironwood**|Lumber Baron, Town Leader|Booming voice, impeccably dressed in fine furs, projects authority and generosity.|**Secret:** The hidden leader of the local Cult of the Dragon cell. A true believer.|
|**Captain Rennick**|Captain of the Town Guard|Grim, tired, and deeply cynical. Wants to keep the peace but is underfunded and pressured.|Knows something is wrong in town but can't prove it. Is being blackmailed by Borin over a past mistake.|
|**Elara**|Head Cartographer, Scholar|Bright, curious, and endlessly enthusiastic about the local flora and history.|Has maps pointing to the hidden Duskwood grove; doesn't know its significance to the cult.|
|**Gareth**|Head Logger|Burly, calloused hands, the informal leader of the common loggers.|Distrusts Borin intensely. Believes the recent "accidents" in the forest were no accident.|
|**Anya**|Borin's Personal Assistant|Quiet, efficient, and always taking notes. Seems utterly loyal.|**Secret:** A Copper Dragon in disguise, observing the cult's activities. Hints at the truth through cryptic advice.|
|**Brother Donovan**|Local Priest of Chauntea|Gentle, kind, and concerned for the people's spiritual and physical well-being.|Has been having visions of a "black shadow" choking the forest, but dismisses them as bad dreams.|
|**"Silent" Sam**|Retired Logger|Doesn't speak, communicates with grunts and gestures. Runs a woodcarving stall.|Was present when the Duskwood grove was found. His tongue was cut out to keep the secret.|
|**Lyssa**|Barmaid at The Splintered Axe|Quick-witted, observant, and the town's unofficial gossip hub.|Flirts with guards to get information, which she sells to the highest bidder.|
|**Korbin**|Owner of The Splintered Axe|A large, jovial man with a magnificent beard. Loves a good story and a strong drink.|His ale is watered down, but his "private stock" is the real deal, saved for special customers.|
|**Thalia**|Owner of The Green Leaf (Herbalist)|Pragmatic, no-nonsense, has a cure for most wood-related ailments.|Is secretly growing a small patch of rare, magical herbs that the cult needs for their rituals.|
|**Dravin**|Blacksmith|Soot-covered and perpetually grumpy about the quality of iron shipped to him.|Is secretly crafting non-lethal, manacle-like restraints for Borin, on orders for "captive lumber."|
|**Finn**|Young Orphan|Nimble, sneaky, and knows all the best hiding spots in town.|Steals small items from rich merchants and leaves them for the poor. Saw cultists meeting at night.|
|**Marlen**|Head of the Ironwood Loggers (Lieutenant)|Intensely loyal to Borin, cold eyes, carries two handaxes.|**Secret:** Borin's enforcer and a devout cultist. Carries out sabotage and intimidation.|
|**Old Lady Agatha**|Seamstress|Sweet, grandmotherly, loves to feed people pastries.|Is completely deaf but an expert lip-reader. Knows more secrets than anyone.|
|**Pike**|Riverboat Ferryman|Grizzled, smokes a foul-smelling pipe, knows the river currents like the back of his hand.|Smuggles small, unknown packages for Borin downriver, no questions asked.|

#### **Locations & Shops**

|Building Name|Owner|Description|
|---|---|---|
|**The Splintered Axe**|Korbin|The town's main inn and tavern. Raucous, filled with off-duty loggers. The floors are permanently covered in a layer of sawdust.|
|**Ironwood Lumber Mill**|Borin Ironwood|The heart of the town. A large, noisy complex on the river where logs are processed. The off-limits office building is where the cult plots.|
|**The Green Leaf Apothecary**|Thalia|A small shop filled with the smell of drying herbs and pine resin. Sells healing potions, antitoxins, and remedies for saw-blister.|
|**Dravin's Forge**|Dravin|A hot, loud smithy specializing in axes, saw blades, horseshoes, and hardware for logging.|
|**The Sturdy Pack**|_Orin_|A general store selling ropes, rations, boots, and other adventuring and logging supplies.|
|**Kirthaven Chapel**|Brother Donovan|A simple, wooden chapel dedicated to Chauntea, the goddess of agriculture. A place of quiet solace.|
|**The Silent Carving**|"Silent" Sam|A small market stall selling beautifully carved wooden figurines of forest animals.|
|**Ironwood Estate**|Borin Ironwood|A large, newly constructed manor house on a hill overlooking the town. Heavily guarded.|
|**The Logger's Repose**|_Unaffiliated_|A simple bunkhouse for transient loggers. Cheap, loud, and not very clean.|

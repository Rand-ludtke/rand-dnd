> A town of sawdust and suspicion, where the hum of industry masks a whispered heresy.

#### **Overview**

- **Population:** ~850 permanent residents, plus ~150 transient loggers and merchants.

---

#### **Notable NPCs**

| Name                | Role                                      | Personality / Gimmick                                                                      |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Borin Ironwood**  | Lumber Baron, Town Leader                 | Booming voice, impeccably dressed in fine furs, projects authority and generosity.         |
| **Captain Rennick** | Captain of the Town Guard                 | Grim, tired, and deeply cynical. Wants to keep the peace but is underfunded and pressured. |
| **Elara**           | Head Cartographer, Scholar                | Bright, curious, and endlessly enthusiastic about the local flora and history.             |
| **Gareth**          | Head Logger                               | Burly, calloused hands, the informal leader of the common loggers.                         |
| **Anya**            | Borin's Personal Assistant                | Quiet, efficient, and always taking notes. Seems utterly loyal.                            |
| **Brother Donovan** | Local Priest of Chauntea                  | Gentle, kind, and concerned for the people's spiritual and physical well-being.            |
| **"Silent" Sam**    | Retired Logger                            | Doesn't speak, communicates with grunts and gestures. Runs a woodcarving stall.            |
| **Lyssa**           | Barmaid at The Splintered Axe             | Quick-witted, observant, and the town's unofficial gossip hub.                             |
| **Korbin**          | Owner of The Splintered Axe               | A large, jovial man with a magnificent beard. Loves a good story and a strong drink.       |
| **Thalia**          | Owner of The Green Leaf (Herbalist)       | Pragmatic, no-nonsense, has a cure for most wood-related ailments.                         |
| **Dravin**          | Blacksmith                                | Soot-covered and perpetually grumpy about the quality of iron shipped to him.              |
| **Finn**            | Young Orphan                              | Nimble, sneaky, and knows all the best hiding spots in town.                               |
| **Marlen**          | Head of the Ironwood Loggers (Lieutenant) | Intensely loyal to Borin, cold eyes, carries two handaxes.                                 |
| **Old Lady Agatha** | Seamstress                                | Sweet, grandmotherly, loves to feed people pastries.                                       |
| **Pike**            | Riverboat Ferryman                        | Grizzled, smokes a foul-smelling pipe, knows the river currents like the back of his hand. |

#### **Locations & Shops**

|Building Name|Owner|Description|
|---|---|---|
|**The Splintered Axe**|Korbin|The town's main inn and tavern. Raucous, filled with off-duty loggers. The floors are permanently covered in a layer of sawdust.|
|**Ironwood Lumber Mill**|Borin Ironwood|The heart of the town. A large, noisy complex on the river where logs are processed. The off-limits office building is where the cult plots.|
|**The Green Leaf Apothecary**|Thalia|A small shop filled with the smell of drying herbs and pine resin. Sells healing potions, antitoxins, and remedies for saw-blister.|
|**Dravin's Forge**|Dravin|A hot, loud smithy specializing in axes, saw blades, horseshoes, and hardware for logging.|
|**The Sturdy Pack**|_Orin_|A general store selling ropes, rations, boots, and other adventuring and logging supplies.|
|**Kirthaven Chapel**|Brother Donovan|A simple, wooden chapel dedicated to Chauntea, the goddess of agriculture. A place of quiet solace.|
|**The Silent Carving**|"Silent" Sam|A small market stall selling beautifully carved wooden figurines of forest animals.|
|**Ironwood Estate**|Borin Ironwood|A large, newly constructed manor house on a hill overlooking the town. Heavily guarded.|
|**The Logger's Repose**|_Unaffiliated_|A simple bunkhouse for transient loggers. Cheap, loud, and not very clean.|

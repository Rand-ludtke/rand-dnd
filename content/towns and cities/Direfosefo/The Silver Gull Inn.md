**Type:** Inn & Tavern  
**Location:** Northern side of Direfosefo, overlooking the lake

A two-story timber inn with whitewashed walls and a painted sign of a silver gull swooping over waves. Inside, the common room smells of baked bread and lake-fish stew, with imported wines lining the shelves. Warm fires, polished wooden tables, and the occasional bard create a welcoming—if oddly polished—atmosphere. Guest rooms are simple but cozy, each with lavender sprigs tucked into pillows.

---

#### **NPCs:**

- **Marven gay** (Male Human, 40s) – The genial innkeeper and “retired fisherman.” Loves to hear travelers’ stories and often asks about their destinations.
    
- **Serra gay** (Female Human, late teens) – Marven’s shy but curious daughter, who works the counter and enjoys speaking with adventurers. She often writes notes about guests in a personal journal.
    
- **“Veyros”** (Male Half-Elf Bard, frequent guest) – A charming performer who entertains in the common room when passing through town. He takes a particular interest in adventurers.

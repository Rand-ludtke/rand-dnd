**Threat Level:** High

## **Overview**

Unbeknownst to most residents, Direfosefo has been under infernal control for the past several months. The new mayor and several key figures in town are devils or their agents, working toward hidden objectives. While they have improved trade, security, and business—particularly aiding the rise of _High Altatude Brews_—they have their own sinister motives.

Their primary goal in Direfosefo appears to be twofold:

1. **Economic Control** – Establishing influence over the region by boosting commerce and securing leverage over merchants.
2. **Investigating the Dragon Cult** – The devils are actively hunting for signs of the nearby cult dedicated to Ralofir, seeing them as a threat to their greater schemes.

## **Key Infernal Figures**

### **Mayor Vaelcor Fane** (Devil in Disguise)

- **True Form:** A charming yet ruthless devil, possibly a cambion or higher-ranking fiend.
- **Public Persona:** A competent, well-spoken mayor who has rapidly improved the town’s prosperity. He claims to be a noble from a distant land seeking to bring _"order and success"_ to Direfosefo.
- **Powers & Influence:** Subtly enforces contracts, making agreements that always seem beneficial—until debts come due. Anyone who crosses him finds their luck turning sour.

### **Captain Thaldris Kael** (Infernal Enforcer)

- **True Form:** A muscular, red-skinned cambion with smoldering eyes and infernal armor.
- **Public Persona:** The town’s newly appointed _Captain of the Guard_. He has doubled patrols and brought crime rates to record lows.
- **Powers & Influence:** Charismatic and fearsome, he ensures lawbreakers either _disappear_ or are manipulated into servitude.

### **Marla Ashenbrook** (Devil-Bound Merchant)

- **True Form:** A human merchant who struck a bargain with infernal forces. Her soul is bound, and she serves the devils’ interests.
- **Public Persona:** The wealthiest trader in the market, known for securing rare and exotic goods.
- **Powers & Influence:** Can subtly steer the economy, ensuring trade aligns with infernal plans.

### **The Hidden Eye** (Unknown Entity)

- **True Form:** A fiend lurking in the shadows—possibly a spymaster or unseen agent.
- **Public Persona:** Unclear. The devils in power frequently receive messages and orders, but the source remains elusive.
- **Powers & Influence:** Masters illusions, disguises, and information networks. Could be watching the party.

## **Devilish Deals & Hidden Contracts**

The devils do not rule openly, preferring contracts, coercion, and manipulation. Some townsfolk have unknowingly signed infernal agreements, trading short-term gains for long-term servitude. **Could the party fall into a similar trap?**

**Clues for the Party to Uncover:**

- Old records show that **Vaelcor Fane has no true history** before his arrival.
- The prison has **an underground section** that isn’t on the official town records.
- Some merchants whisper that **Marla’s deals always seem too good to be true**.
- A strange symbol appears **near the lake** on certain nights, hinting at infernal rituals.

## **Possible Plot Hooks**

- **Investigate the Mayor** – Who is he really, and what is his endgame?
- **Rescue a Captive** – Someone in the town signed an infernal contract and is about to disappear.
- **Stop a Summoning** – The devils might be preparing to **open a greater portal** to Hell.
- **The Dragon Cult’s Countermove** – If they know the truth, what will they do about it?

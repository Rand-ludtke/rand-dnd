**Type:** Government Building  
**Location:** Central Direfosefo

The administrative heart of Direfosefo, the Town Hall serves as a place for public meetings, trade negotiations, and town record-keeping. A sturdy, well-maintained stone building with a large meeting chamber and multiple offices, it is the seat of local governance, where the town’s mayor and officials handle disputes and manage development. A large iron bell atop the hall is rung in times of emergency.

#### **NPCs:**

- **Mayor Renald Valtorin** (Male Human) – A fair but firm leader, Mayor Valtorin prioritizes trade and infrastructure, always seeking ways to improve Direfosefo’s reputation.
- **Clerk Adelyne Briarwood** (Female Halfling) – Oversees records, taxes, and letters of business; an incredibly organized and detail-oriented woman.
- **Councilor Orlan Hess** (Male Dwarf) – Represents the town’s craftsmen and traders, advocating for market expansion.
- **Magistrate Elric Thorne** (Male Half-Elf) – Handles legal matters and minor disputes, known for his impartiality.
- **Scribe Ilwen Porthos** (Female Gnome) – Keeps town records, drafts letters, and manages correspondence with Goldfall and Ghostfall.

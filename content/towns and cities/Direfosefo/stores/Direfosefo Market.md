**Location:** Town Center, Direfosefo  
**Type:** Open-Air Market

A bustling hub for travelers and locals alike, the market offers fresh produce, crafted goods, and supplies. The stalls range from food vendors selling local delicacies to blacksmiths, tailors, and potion brewers. The scent of grilled fish, fresh herbs, and spiced pastries fills the air as merchants call out their daily specials.

## **Market Atmosphere & Events**

- **Weekly Bard Performances** – Every few days, traveling musicians stop by to perform in the market square.
- **Festival Market Days** – Occasionally, the market swells with even more exotic wares from visiting traders.
- **Merchant Gossip** – NPCs share rumors about the road ahead, lost items, or strange happenings in the region.

## **NPCs**

1. **Patsy Alta** (Female Elf)
    - Co-owner of the café, Patsy is a talented barista and herbalist. She has a warm and friendly demeanor, always ready with a smile and a perfectly brewed cup of coffee.
2. **Calluway Alta** (Male Elf)
    - Patsy’s husband and co-owner, Calluway manages the business side of the café. He is a skilled baker and takes pride in creating pastries that pair perfectly with their drinks.
3. **Lana Embervale** (Female Half-Elf)
    - A cheerful and quick-footed waitress who always has a joke ready for customers. She enjoys experimenting with new drink flavors and often offers customers “off-menu” blends.
4. **Otis Bramblewood** (Male Halfling)
    - A meticulous and attentive server who prides himself on remembering regulars’ orders. He has a passion for tea and is always eager to recommend the perfect pairing.
5. **Mariella "Mari" Voss** (Female Tiefling)
    - A flirty and energetic waitress who thrives in the café’s lively environment. She’s known for creating beautifully plated pastries and giving artistic foam designs in coffee.

## **Flavor & Atmosphere**

- The café has a cozy wooden interior with large windows facing the lake. The scent of freshly brewed coffee and warm pastries fills the air.
- A chalkboard near the entrance lists the daily specials, and handwritten notes from travelers are pinned to a message board.
- Soft music plays from an enchanted lute in the corner, while the buzz of conversation and the clinking of cups create a lively yet comfortable ambiance.
- The outdoor patio offers a peaceful view of the lake, making it a favorite spot for locals and travelers alike.

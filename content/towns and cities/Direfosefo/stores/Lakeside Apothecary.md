**Type:** Herbalist Shop  
**Location:** Direfosefo Market

A wooden stall decorated with hanging bundles of dried herbs and small bottles filled with mysterious liquids. A faint herbal scent drifts through the air as customers browse through potions, salves, and natural remedies.

#### **NPCs:**

- **Ilyana Mossbrook** (Female Elf) – A calm and wise herbalist who knows every plant in the region. She is meticulous in her craft and enjoys helping those in need.
- **Fennic “Fen” Walloway** (Male Gnome) – An energetic assistant who always seems to have dirt under his fingernails from foraging fresh ingredients.

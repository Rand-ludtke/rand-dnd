**Type:** General Goods Store  
**Location:** Direfosefo Market

A staple for travelers and locals alike, the general store provides all the essentials one might need for a journey or daily life. The shelves are stocked with food, tools, and basic adventuring supplies. A wooden counter displays a small selection of valuables under glass, while a wall of sturdy backpacks and bedrolls hangs in the back.

#### **NPCs:**

- **Hugo Fenwick** (Male Human) – The friendly and slightly scatterbrained shopkeeper who has been running the store for years. He enjoys haggling but is fair with his prices.
- **Mira Fenwick** (Female Human) – Hugo’s sharp-witted wife, who actually runs the store’s books and ensures everything stays organized.

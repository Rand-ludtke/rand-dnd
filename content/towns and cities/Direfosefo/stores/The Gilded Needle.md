**Type:** Tailor’s Shop  
**Location:** Direfosefo Market

A quaint shop adorned with fabric rolls, mannequins dressed in finely made garments, and shelves lined with spools of thread in every imaginable color. The scent of fresh linen and leather lingers in the air as seamstresses work on their latest commissions.

#### **NPCs:**

- **Madame Vivienne Lorrain** (Female Elf) – A master tailor known for her refined taste and perfectionism. She believes that clothing is not just fabric but a statement of character.
- **Thom "Patch" Berrick** (Male Halfling) – A cheerful and fast-working apprentice, skilled in mending and quick alterations.

**Type:** Blacksmith’s Forge  
**Location:** Direfosefo Market

The sound of clanging metal echoes through the market as the forge burns hot. The shop smells of iron, soot, and oil, with weapons and armor on display alongside more practical goods like horseshoes and farming tools.

#### **NPCs:**

- **Garrik Ironhand** (Male Dwarf) – The no-nonsense owner, known for his skill in forging sturdy, reliable equipment.
- **Rella Stoneshine** (Female Human) – A blacksmith’s apprentice, enthusiastic and eager to prove herself.

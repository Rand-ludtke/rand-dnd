**Location:** A section of the lake near Direfosefo
![[Pasted image 20250221170812.png]]

On certain nights, especially under a full moon, this part of the lake glows with an ethereal, otherworldly shimmer. Some claim the effect is caused by unique minerals, while others believe it to be the lingering touch of ancient magic. The water feels unusually smooth and slightly warm to the touch, and some say drinking from it brings vivid dreams or momentary flashes of forgotten memories.

#### **What You Can Do Here:**

- **Night Fishing** – The glow attracts fish that are normally deep in the lake.
- **Meditation & Reflection** – Some claim sitting by the pool enhances clarity of mind.
- **Water Collection** – The shimmer fades when removed but is prized for rituals and alchemy.
- **Rumored Enchantment** – Legends speak of lost items or spirits appearing in the pool’s reflection.

#### **Lake Life Found Here:**

- **Silverfin Trout** – A shimmering, fast-moving fish that’s difficult to catch but delicious.
- **Glow Koi** – Large fish with faintly luminous scales, thought to be a sign of good luck.
- **Moon Eels** – Slender, slippery creatures that hide beneath the water’s glow.
- **Starburst Jellyfish** – Tiny, bioluminescent jellies that drift beneath the surface.
- **Mossback Crabs** – Hardy freshwater crabs that cling to submerged rocks.

The Shimmering Pool is a popular yet mysterious attraction, drawing visitors, scholars, and the occasional adventurer looking for something hidden beneath the waves.

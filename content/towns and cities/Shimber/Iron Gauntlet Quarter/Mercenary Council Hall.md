### **Additional Features**

- **Experienced Mercenaries**: All mercenaries are **highly trained and experienced** in their respective fields.
    
- **Magical Support**: Some mercenaries are **skilled in magic**, providing additional support during missions.
    
- **Global Network**: Access to a **global network** of mercenaries, traders, and adventurers.
    
- **Exclusive Discounts**: Members of the **Mercenary Guild** receive **exclusive discounts** on all services.

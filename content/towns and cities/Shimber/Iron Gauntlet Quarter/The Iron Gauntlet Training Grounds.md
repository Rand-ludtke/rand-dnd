### **Additional Features**

- **Experienced Trainers**: All trainers are **highly skilled and experienced** in their respective fields.
    
- **Magical Support**: Some trainers are **skilled in magic**, providing additional support during training.
    
- **Global Network**: Access to a **global network** of trainers, adventurers, and warriors.
    
- **Exclusive Discounts**: Members of the **Training Facility** receive **exclusive discounts** on all programs.

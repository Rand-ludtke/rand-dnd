## **NPCs**

1. **Sister Maribel** (Human, Female)
    
    - A kind-hearted cleric who tends the fountain's shrine. She offers blessings to visitors and listens patiently to their troubles. Always carries a silver watering can to tend the flowers.
        
2. **Old Tom** (Halfling, Male)
    
    - The fountain's self-appointed caretaker, who scrubs the stones daily and knows all the local gossip. Sells "lucky coins" (ordinary copper pieces) to toss in the fountain.
        
3. **Liriel Dawnsong** (Elf, Female)
    
    - A melancholic bard who plays harp by the fountain each evening. Her songs subtly reveal hidden truths about the city’s nobility.

## **Flavor**

- The fountain’s centerpiece is a marble statue of a weeping angel, its cascading water glowing faintly under moonlight. Pigeons and songbirds nest in its alcoves, and the scent of jasmine lingers in the air.
    
- **Rumor:** Locals whisper that the angel’s tears turn to gold on the summer solstice—but only for those pure of heart.
    
- **Quest Hook:** Liriel begs the party to investigate why the fountain’s waters have begun tasting metallic. (Spoiler: A rust monster has nested in the aqueducts.)

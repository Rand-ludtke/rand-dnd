**Location:** The Blessed Gardens
**Type:** Park & Meditation Spot

## **NPCs**

1. **Elira Whisperleaf** (Elf, Female)
    
    - A serene druid who tends the garden's magical plants. She speaks in riddles and offers wisdom to visitors. Known for her ability to commune with wind spirits.
        
2. **Tobin Breezefoot** (Halfling, Male)
    
    - A cheerful groundskeeper who maintains the garden paths. Always humming and leaving small wind chimes as gifts for kind visitors.
        
3. **Zephyra the Listener** (Air Genasi, Nonbinary)
    
    - A mystic who meditates in the garden’s heart. They sell enchanted items and interpret the "whispers of the wind" for a price.

## **Flavor**

- **Atmosphere:** The garden is a symphony of rustling leaves and wind chimes. Petals drift lazily on perpetual breezes, and the air smells of rain and lavender. Stone paths spiral around ancient oaks, their branches bent into graceful arches by centuries of wind.
    
- **Rumor:** Locals claim the garden’s central oak is a dormant **treant** that awakens during storms to protect the area.
    
- **Quest Hook:** Zephyra seeks a "song lost to the east wind"—a melody they believe is key to unlocking a **hidden air elemental shrine**.

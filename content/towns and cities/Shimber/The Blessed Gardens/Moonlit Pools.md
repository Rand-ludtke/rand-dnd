**Type:** Romantic Garden with Reflecting Pools

## **NPCs**

1. **Eliara Moonshadow** (Elf, Female)
    
    - A serene garden keeper who tends to the pools. She speaks in whispers and believes the moonlight has healing properties. Offers guided meditations.
        
2. **Dorin Brightwater** (Halfling, Male)
    
    - A jovial florist selling enchanted blossoms. Always has a story about lovers who met at the pools. Secretly a matchmaker.

## **Flavor**

- **Atmosphere:** The air hums with the scent of night-blooming jasmine. Silver koi dart through pools that mirror the constellations above. Couples whisper promises under arched trellises woven with glowing vines.
    
- **Rumor:** Locals say if you kiss someone under the "Twin Moons" reflection (a rare optical illusion), your souls will be bound for a year and a day.
    
- **Quest Hook:** Eliara seeks a rare **Midnight Orchid** to cure the pools’ fading magic—but it only grows in the Shadowfell.

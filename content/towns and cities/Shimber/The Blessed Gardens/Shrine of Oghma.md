## **NPCs**

1. **High Scribe Eldron** (Elf, Male)
    
    - **Role:** Head priest, keeper of sacred texts.
        
    - **Personality:** Stern but patient, speaks in riddles. Believes "a question unasked is a door unopened."
        
    - **Quirk:** Always carries a quill that never runs out of ink.
        
2. **Saria Quillheart** (Human, Female)
    
    - **Role:** Archivist and translator.
        
    - **Personality:** Bubbly but obsessive about organizing knowledge. Hums while shelving books.
        
    - **Quirk:** Her eyes glow faintly when she reads ancient scripts.

## **Flavor & Features**

- **The Hall of Whispers:** A library where books rearrange themselves at night. Speaking too loudly earns a glare from spectral librarians.
    
- **The Inkwell Altar:** A pool of black liquid that reflects questions as floating text. Drinking it grants **one truthful answer** (but costs 1d4 INT damage).
    
- **Pilgrim’s Ritual:** Visitors must **write a secret** in the shrine’s ledger to enter. Lies make the ink burn their fingers.
    
- **Living Tomes:** Tiny, bat-like constructs that flutter to fetch requested books. They bite thieves.
    

**Rumor:** A forbidden section holds a book that **erases the reader’s name** from reality. Saria won’t admit it exists.

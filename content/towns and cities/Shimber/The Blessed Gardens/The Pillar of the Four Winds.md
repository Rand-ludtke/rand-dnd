### **NPCs**

1. **High Priestess Zephyra** (Air Genasi, Female)
    
    - A serene figure with swirling cloud-like hair who oversees rituals. She speaks in riddles and believes "the wind carries truths mortals cannot grasp."
        
2. **Brother Ember** (Fire Genasi, Male)
    
    - A passionate caretaker of the sacred braziers. His temper flares like the flames he tends, but his loyalty to the church is unshakable.
        
3. **Sister Maris** (Water Genasi, Female)
    
    - A healer who uses blessed water to cure ailments. Her voice sounds like a babbling brook, and she’s endlessly patient.
        
4. **Stoneheart Varek** (Earth Genasi, Male)
    
    - The silent, stoic guardian of the church’s relics. His words are rare but weighty, like stones dropping into a pond.

### **Flavor**

- **Atmosphere:** The church is an open-air sanctuary with four towering pillars representing each element. The air hums with distant chimes, braziers flicker without fuel, and a shallow stream winds through polished stone floors. Devotees meditate on floating platforms suspended by unseen winds.
    
- **Rumor:** Locals whisper that the church’s central pillar hides a **portal to the Elemental Planes**, opened only during storms.
    
- **Quest Hook:** Brother Ember seeks brave souls to recover a stolen **"Ember of the Phoenix"** from a cult that plans to extinguish it—and "unmake fire itself."

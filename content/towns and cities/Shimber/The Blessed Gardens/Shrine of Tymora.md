**Location:** The Blessed Gardens
**Type:** Church (Goddess of Luck)

---

## **NPCs**

1. **Sister Liora** (Halfling, Female)
    
    - A cheerful, silver-haired priestess who greets visitors with honeyed cakes and cryptic blessings. Always carries a deck of cards "for fortune-telling" (though she mostly uses them to play pranks).
        
2. **Brother Dain** (Human, Male)
    
    - A former gambler turned acolyte, now devoted to teaching moderation. Secretly keeps a loaded dice set "as a reminder of his past."

## **Flavor**

- **Atmosphere:** The shrine is a sunlit pavilion draped in cobalt and gold silks, where the air smells of citrus and incense. Dice games are played openly on the altar ("Tymora favors boldness!").
    
- **Rumor:** A rogue claims the shrine’s "Lucky Fountain" is rigged—but those who investigate vanish for exactly 24 hours, returning with inexplicable wealth (and no memory).
    
- **Quest Hook:** Sister Liora begs the party to recover **Tymora’s Stolen Coin**, a relic that "tips fate." The thief? A _disgruntled cleric of Beshaba_ hiding in a high-stakes gambling den.

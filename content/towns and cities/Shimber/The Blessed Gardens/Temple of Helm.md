### **NPCs**

1. **Brother Sten** (Human, Male)
    
    - **Head priest.** A gaunt, silver-haired man with piercing blue eyes. Stern but compassionate, he offers sanctuary to the desperate and judges petitioners with Helm’s unwavering fairness.
        
    - _Quote:_ "Helm’s gaze never wavers. Neither shall we."
        
2. **Thara Ironshield** (Dwarf, Female)
    
    - **Guardian of the temple.** A broad-shouldered dwarf clad in burnished plate armor. She trains acolytes in defensive tactics and inspects every visitor for hidden weapons.
        
    - _Quote:_ "Trust is earned. Protection is given."

### **Flavor & Features**

- **Fortified Walls:** The temple’s stone walls are carved with **protective runes** that glow faintly at dusk. Archers patrol the parapets.
    
- **The Watcher’s Altar:** A statue of Helm, his visor lowered, stands atop a dais. Those who touch it feel **a surge of resolve** (advantage on next Wisdom save).
    
- **Echoes of the Past:** In the crypts below, faint **ethereal footsteps** can be heard—said to be Helm walking the halls during the Godswar.
    
- **Strict Rules:** Weapons must be peace-bonded, and all visitors are **watched** by silent sentinels in alcoves.

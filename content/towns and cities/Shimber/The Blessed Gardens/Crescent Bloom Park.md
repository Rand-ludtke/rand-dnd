**Location:** The Blessed Gardens
**Type:** Park (Night Blooming Flowers)

## **NPCs**

1. **Elara Moonshadow** (Eladrin Elf, Female)
    
    - The park's caretaker, a druid who tends to the magical flora. She speaks in poetic riddles and offers moonlit tours. Wears a cloak woven from living vines that bloom after sunset.
        
2. **Thistle "Grumbles" Oakheart** (Gnome, Male)
    
    - A grumpy but kind-hearted botanist who sells rare seeds and complains about "reckless adventurers trampling his flowerbeds." Always carries a magnifying glass and a pouch of glowing pollen.
        
3. **Lucian the Dreamer** (Human, Male)
    
    - A wandering bard who performs lullabies under the moonflowers. Claims the park’s blossoms "whisper secrets" to him.

## **Flavor**

- **Atmosphere:** The park glows faintly after dusk, with bioluminescent petals unfurling to the rhythm of crickets. Silver moths flit between blossoms, and the air smells of jasmine and damp earth. Benches carved from fossilized wood seem to hum when touched.
    
- **Rumor:** Locals whisper that the "Midnight Bloom" (a legendary flower) appears once a decade—whoever finds it gains **one wish**, but at the cost of their deepest memory.
    
- **Quest Hook:** Elara seeks brave souls to retrieve **stolen moonpetals** from a pixie thief hiding in the Feywild.

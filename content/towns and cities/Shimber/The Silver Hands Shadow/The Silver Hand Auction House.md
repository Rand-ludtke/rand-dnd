### **NPCs**

1. **Orvin Greaves** (Elf, Male)
    
    - **Role:** Master Auctioneer
        
    - **Description:** A silver-tongued elf with emerald-green eyes and a penchant for theatrics. His tailored suits hide daggers, and his smile hides darker deals.
        
    - **Trait:** Knows every fence in the city. Charges a 15% "discretion fee" for stolen goods.
        
    - **Hook:** Offers PCs "off-the-books" jobs to retrieve high-value items.
        
2. **Lady Tenebris** (Human, Female)
    
    - **Role:** Enigmatic Collector
        
    - **Description:** Always veiled in black lace, her voice is a whisper. Rumors say she’s a vampire or a warlock—no one dares ask.
        
    - **Trait:** Pays triple for cursed objects. Her bodyguards are silent, armored figures.
        
    - **Hook:** Seeks a _"living shadow"_ for her collection—will pay 10,000 gp.

### **Services**

1. **Discreet Appraisals** (50 gp): Orvin values items _without_ asking questions.
    
2. **Lottery of Fate** (10 gp/ticket): Gamble on unopened vaults (could contain treasure or traps).
    
3. **Bidding Proxy** (200 gp): A masked agent bids for you to avoid attention.
    
4. **Debt Collection** (20% cut): The Silver Hand "recovers" unpaid dues—violently.

### **Flavor & Secrets**

- **The Back Room:** A hidden lounge where criminals trade favors. Membership requires a "blood signature."
    
- **Last Season’s Scandal:** A bidder vanished after winning the _Horn of the Fallen Celestial_. His corpse was found grinning, eyes full of starlight.
    
- **Rumor:** Lady Tenebris is assembling a _"Gallery of the Damned"_—living art trapped in cursed frames.

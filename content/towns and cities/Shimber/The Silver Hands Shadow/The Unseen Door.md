**Location:** The Silver Hand’s Shadow  
**Type:** Entertainment (Secret Casino)

---

### **NPCs**

1. **Vellin Crux** (Human, Male)
    
    - **Description:** A sharply dressed man with oiled-back hair and a perpetual smirk. His velvet gloves hide scars from "handling" cheaters.
        
    - **Role:** Casino owner. Offers high-stakes games with a velvet-gloved iron fist.
        
    - **Quote:** _"The House always wins... but tonight, you might be the exception."_
        
2. **Ziri Frostfang** (Dragonborn, Female)
    
    - **Description:** Sapphire-blue scales and piercing silver eyes. Her claws tap impatiently on card decks.
        
    - **Role:** Card dealer. Spots cheats with her **Keen Mind** trait (remembers every card played).
        
    - **Quote:** _"Blink twice if you’re about to regret your choices."_
	    
3. **"Madame Silk"** (Changeling, Female)
    
    - **Role:** Oversees **high-stakes psychic gambling**. Her table is draped in **memory-silk** (steals secrets from losers).
        
    - **Gimmick:** Her games use **living tarot cards** that scream your fate.
        
4. **Grimtooth** (Goblin, Male)
    
    - **Role:** Runs the **back-alley dice games**. Offers "insurance" (i.e., sabotage) for a cut of winnings.
        
    - **Gimmick:** His dice are **knuckle bones of a lich** (roll a 1? You age 1d4 years).

#### **The Darkest Games **

##### **1. The Hunger Table**

- Gamble using **starvation tokens** (1 token = 1 day without food).
    
- Winners get a **feast of illusory food**; losers take **1d20 necrotic damage**.
    
- _Special Rule:_ If you die, your corpse becomes **casino decor**.
    

##### **2. The Blood Wheel**

- Spin a wheel of **razor blades**.
    
- Where it lands, you **lose that body part** (roll on a limb-loss table).
    
- _Payout:_ Replacement limbs (quality varies).
    

##### **3. The Silent Auction**

- Bid using **your voice** (1 hour = 100 gp, permanently = 10,000 gp).
    
- Winners receive **a secret from the Void**.
    
- _Warning:_ Some secrets **cannot be unlearned**.

A **black obelisk** manned by a **mummified accountant** named _Dusty_. Exchange **anything** for chips:

| Item Sacrificed                          | Chip Value (gp) | Side Effect                                                                  |
| ---------------------------------------- | --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 Gold Coin                              | 1               | —                                                                            |
| A Memory (1 hour)                        | 100             | Roll a d20: On 1, forget _your own name_ for 1d4 hours.                      |
| A Finger                                 | 500             | Permanent -1 to Sleight of Hand.                                             |
| Your Shadow                              | 1,000           | Creatures have **advantage** on Perception checks to see you.                |
| A Year of Your Life(1% of true lifespan) | 5,000           | You age visibly. and lose 1D4 Con.                                           |
| Your True Name                           | 10,000          | _Vellin now owns your soul contract._                                        |
| **Your Soul**                            | 100,000         | You become a **casino employee** for eternity. _No save._                    |
| **Your Reflection**                      | 7,500           | You cast no mirror image. _Doppelgängers ignore you._                        |
| **Your Laughter**                        | 12,000          | You can no longer laugh. _Advantage on Intimidation._                        |
| **Your Dreams**                          | 25,000          | You no longer sleep. _Immune to psychic damage but gain indefinite madness._ |
| **Your Legacy**                          | 50,000          | No one remembers your past. _You are a stranger to all._                     |
| **Your Death**                           | 100,000         | You cannot die. _You also cannot heal._                                      |
| Organs                                   | Varies          | Varies                                                                       |

- Cashing out requires a **Charisma save (DC 15)** to resist gambling them all back.
    
- If you cash out **under -1,000,000 gp in chips**, the casino **owns your afterlife**.

## **The Lich’s Last Hand (Forbidden Game)**

**Location:** A **floating skull table** in the casino’s basement, accessible only via _secret elevator_.  
**Dealer:** **Xal'thur the Eternal**, a **lich** who gambled his phylactery and lost.

### **Rules:**

- Played with **a deck of skin-bound cards**.
    
- Each player antes **one irreversible life choice** (e.g., "I never fall in love again").
    
- Winning hand **rewrites reality** in a 1-mile radius for 24 hours.
    

### **Possible Outcomes:**

|Hand|Effect|
|---|---|
|**Royal Flush**|_You become the casino’s owner._ (Vellin becomes your servant.)|
|**Five of a Kind**|_One law of physics ceases to exist_ (DM’s choice).|
|**Dead Man’s Hand**|_You and Xal'thur switch bodies for 1d10 days._|

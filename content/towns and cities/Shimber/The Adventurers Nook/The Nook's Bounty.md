## Flavor

- The shop is **warm and inviting**, with wooden shelves filled with colorful potions, dried herbs, maps, and adventuring gear.
    
- The walls are decorated with **trophies from Tilda's travels**, including exotic weapons and preserved creatures.
    
- Adventurers often gather here to **share news, seek advice, and stock up on supplies** before setting out on their quests.
    
- Tilda is known for her **friendly demeanor** and willingness to share stories of her own adventures.

_"In the veins of magical creatures flows power unimaginable, and you, bold or perhaps mad, have sought to claim it for your own. Where others see beasts or myths, you see opportunity. You’ve turned your body into a living crucible, blending your innate sorcery with the blood and essence of creatures born of magic. The price of your ambition may twist your body or unmake your soul, but the rewards are as tempting as they are immense."_

The Bloodbound Sorcerer is a subclass for those who crave power drawn from the lifeblood of magical beings. Infusing yourself with their essence, you gain abilities that reflect the traits and nature of the creatures you consume. However, such experimentation is not without risk—both body and mind can warp under the strain, leaving you with bizarre, unpredictable mutations that can aid or hinder you.

Will you become a beacon of arcane innovation, wielding the strength of dragons and elementals? Or will your hubris lead to your downfall, as your body succumbs to the chaos of unstable magic?

### **Level 3:Essence Infusion**

#### **Harvesting Essence**

To harvest the essence of a magical creature:

1. **Essence Requirements**: The creature must be slain within the last 8 hours and have inherent magical properties (DM discretion).
2. **Skill Check**: A DC 10 Medicine or Arcana check is required to extract a usable vial of essence. Failure destroys the essence.

#### **Infusion Process**

You can infuse essence into your body during a long rest. The infusion process carries inherent risk:

- **Constitution Saving Throw**: When infusing essence, make a Constitution saving throw. The DC is 10 + the rank of the creature's essence.
    - On a failure, roll on the **Bad Magical Mutation Table** and suffer one of its effects permanently.
    - On a success, choose one of the essence's **passive benefit** and choose one **active ability** related to the infused creature.
    - On a natural 20, roll on the **Good Magical Mutation Table** and gain one mutation in addition to the essence effects or remove 1 bad mutation effect that you currently have.
    - **Essence Limits**: You can have 2 * character level number of **passive** benefits, but only a number of **active** abilities equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum 1) can be available at a time(you have x active ability slots that you can fill only 1 can be used at a time). Switching active abilities requires a Short Rest.

#### **Essence Effects**

Each essence grants:

- **One Passive Effect**: A permanent benefit related to the creature (e.g., learning a spell, resistance, skill enhancement).
- **One Active Ability**: A power you can use in combat or exploration. You can maintain a number of active abilities equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum 1).

Switching active abilities requires a Short Rest.

|**Rank**|**Creature Examples**|**Passive Effect Examples**|**Active Ability Examples**|
|---|---|---|---|
|Rank 1|Pixie, Blink Dog, Sprite|Learn a cantrip related to the creature (e.g., _Dancing Lights_, _Thaumaturgy_).|Fire a magical dart (1d6 poison, DC 12 Con save or poisoned).|
|Rank 2|Dryad, Elemental (Small)|Gain limited resistance: Take 3 less damage from one type (fire, cold, etc.).|Release a 15-ft cone of elemental force (2d6 damage of type, DC 13 Dex save).|
|Rank 3|Wyvern, Unicorn, Medusa|Learn a spell such as _Lesser Restoration_ or _Suggestion_.|Poisonous gaze: Action to force a DC 14 Con save or take 4d6 poison damage.|
|Rank 4|Adult Dragon, Aboleth|Resistance to one damage type permanently (fire, acid, psychic, etc.).|Dragon’s breath (60-ft cone, 6d6 damage of chosen type, DC 15 Dex save, 1/day).|
|Rank 5|Kraken, Ancient Dragon|Immunity to restrained underwater, or learn a 4th-level spell.|Transform arms into tentacles (15-ft reach, grapple on hit, DC 16 escape).|

### **Level 6: Essence Resonance**

The bond you forge with magical creatures deepens, allowing their essence to resonate through your spells and abilities. At this level, you refine your control over these mystical energies, wielding them in ways that reflect the power of the creatures you’ve siphoned.

When you gain this feature, you can choose **Resonant Strike** or **Adaptive Essence** to shape your connection with the magic coursing through you. Once chosen, this feature cannot be changed unless your DM allows it through significant events in your journey (such as a major magical ritual or divine intervention).

#### **Resonant Strike**

_The power of your spells surges with the energy of the essences you've absorbed._

Whenever you cast a spell that deals damage, you can choose to infuse it with the essence of a creature you’ve previously absorbed. This resonance manifests in a unique way based on the type of creature. You can use this ability a number of times equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum of 1) per long rest.

Choose one effect from the table below for each type of absorbed essence:

|**Creature Type**|**Resonant Strike Effect**|
|---|---|
|**Aberration**|The spell warps reality. A target takes an extra 1d8 psychic damage, and if it moves before the start of your next turn, it takes 1d6 psychic damage.|
|**Celestial**|Radiant light suffuses your magic. One affected creature of your choice regains hit points equal to your Charisma modifier, and the spell deals an additional 1d8 radiant damage.|
|**Dragon**|Your spell channels draconic might. It deals an extra 1d8 damage of the dragon’s associated element (fire, cold, lightning, acid, or poison).|
|**Elemental**|Raw elemental energy enhances your spell. Add 1d8 damage of the elemental’s associated type (fire, cold, lightning, thunder, etc.) and push one creature 10 feet away from you.|
|**Fey**|Illusions swirl within your spell. Affected creatures must make a Wisdom saving throw against your spell save DC or be charmed by you until the end of your next turn.|
|**Fiend**|Dark energy lashes out. Targets have disadvantage on saving throws against your spell, and you deal an additional 1d8 fire or necrotic damage.|
|**Giant**|Tremors ripple through your spell. The spell deals 1d8 bludgeoning damage to all creatures within 10 feet of the target, as the ground cracks under its force.|
|**Undead**|Necrotic power seeps into your spell. You deal an extra 1d8 necrotic damage, and creatures killed by this spell cannot be raised as undead.|

  
As you unleash your spell, the energy resonates with the creature's essence, reshaping its power to mirror the source. The battlefield feels the echo of draconic roars, celestial hymns, or the warped hum of aberrant energy.

---

#### **Adaptive Essence**

_Your body and mind adapt to the creatures whose essence flows through you._

When you finish a long rest, you can draw out latent energy from one absorbed essence, gaining a passive benefit until your next long rest. Choose one benefit from the table below based on the essence type:

|**Creature Type**|**Adaptive Essence Benefit**|
|---|---|
|**Aberration**|You gain darkvision out to 60 feet, or 120 feet if you already have darkvision. You also gain advantage on saving throws against being frightened or charmed.|
|**Celestial**|You can cast the _guidance_ cantrip and regain hit points equal to your sorcerer level when you succeed on a saving throw once per long rest.|
|**Dragon**|You gain temporary resistance to the dragon’s elemental damage type. In addition, when you succeed on a saving throw against a breath weapon, you can use your reaction to deal 1d6 damage of the same type to the creature.|
|**Elemental**|You can cast the _shape water_, _control flames_, or _mold earth_ cantrip as an innate ability once per long rest. Your movement is unaffected by difficult terrain caused by natural elements (e.g., ice, sand, or lava).|
|**Fey**|You gain proficiency in the Deception or Persuasion skill and advantage on saving throws against being charmed.|
|**Fiend**|You gain resistance to fire damage and advantage on saving throws against being frightened.|
|**Giant**|Your carrying capacity doubles, and you count as one size larger for determining grapples and shoves.|
|**Undead**|You gain immunity to effects that reduce your hit point maximum and resistance to necrotic damage.|

  
Each morning, you feel your body subtly shift as the essence you’ve absorbed flows through your veins. Your footsteps might leave a faint shimmer of frost, or your touch might spark like flame.


### **Level 6: Blood Infusion Mastery**

You have refined control over your infusions:

- Switch active abilities as a bonus action.
- Once per long rest, you can reroll a failed Constitution saving throw to avoid mutation.

### **Level 10: Mutative Arcana**

You embrace the transformative power of magic. Each Rank 4 or 5 infusion grants a roll on the **Good Magical Mutation Table**. You can reroll a mutation once per long rest.

### **Level 14: Creature’s Essence**

You gain greater mastery over the powers of infused creatures:

- Replicate one legendary action or innate ability of a creature whose essence you’ve infused, once per long rest.
- Increase your spell save DC by 2 for 1 minute after using this feature.

### **Level 18: Apex Bloodbound**

You are the apex of magical evolution:

- Maintain two active abilities simultaneously.
- You can infuse Rank 4 and 5 essences without making Constitution saving throws.
- Choose one infused spell. You can cast it at will without expending a spell slot.

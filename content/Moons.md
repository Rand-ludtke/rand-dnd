### Full Moon Names & Significance

|Moon Name|Common Name & Significance|
|---|---|
|**Lunara**|**The Guide's Lantern**  <br>The most common and reliable moon. Its full light is used by travelers and sailors for navigation. A full Lunara is considered a good omen for journeys begun that night.|
|**Selthar**|**The Greenwarden**  <br>Known as the gardener's moon. Its faint greenish hue is said to encourage growth. Planting or harvesting herbs by its light is a common folk practice.|
|**Verenis**|**The Ivory Sentinel**  <br>The brightest and largest moon in the sky. Its full phase is a time for major festivals, royal proclamations, and sacred vows. A night of celebration and momentous decisions.|
|**Mornael**|**The Tidesinger**  <br>This moon has a strong, documented effect on the tides and creatures of the sea. Its full phase is feared by coastal communities, as it often brings fierce storms and high waves.|
|**Aeloria**|**The Weaver's Orb**  <br>Its beautiful, rosy light is associated with artists, poets, and lovers. Inspiration is said to flow more easily under a full Aeloria, but so do intense, sometimes fleeting, passions.|

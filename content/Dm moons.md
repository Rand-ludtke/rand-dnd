### Lunar Intensity Mechanic (High-Stakes):

| Total Intensity  | Control DC | Base State                                                                                 |
| ---------------- | ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **0.0x - 0.25x** | DC 10      | **Control.** Voluntary change only.                                                        |
| **0.5x - 0.75x** | DC 15      | **Control.** Voluntary change only, but the beast is restless.                             |
| **1.0x - 1.25x** | **DC 18**  | **Stirring.** Wisdom save vs involuntary change when injured, angered, or frightened.      |
| **1.5x - 1.75x** | **DC 20**  | **Strain.** Wisdom save at dusk to avoid involuntary transformation for the night.         |
| **2.0x - 2.25x** | **DC 25**  | **Dominance.** Involuntary transformation at night. Wisdom save (DC 25) at dawn to revert. |
| **2.5x+**        | **DC 30+** | **Apogee.** Feral. No save to revert until dawn. No memory of the night's events.          |
### Lycanthrope Empowerment

As lunar intensity increases, the raw power of the transformation grows, but at the cost of the werekin's higher reasoning and control.

| Total Intensity | Power Boost (Cumulative)                 | Mental State                                                                                                          |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1.0x+**       | **+10 Temporary HP**                     | Rational thought remains, but predatory instincts are loud.                                                           |
| **1.5x+**       | **Claw/Weapon attacks deal +1d6 damage** | Language becomes difficult; complex tactics fade. The beast wants to chase and rend.                                  |
| **2.0x+**       | **AC increases by +2**                   | Sapience is a distant memory. The creature operates on pure instinct, focusing on the biggest threat or easiest prey. |
| **2.5x+**       | **All damage dealt is doubled**          | A force of nature. Cannot distinguish friend from foe. Will atta                                                      |
### Lunar Mutations & Side Effects (0.5x Intensity Required)

A moon must contribute at least **0.5x** intensity for its specific mutation to manifest.

| Moon        | Mutation & Combat Effect (Activates at 0.5x Intensity)                                                                                                                                  |
| ----------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Lunara**  | **Predatory Focus:** The werekin can use a Bonus Action to gain advantage on its next attack roll against a creature it can see.                                                        |
| **Selthar** | **Verdant Regeneration:** At the start of its turn, if it is in a natural environment, it regains 1d6 hit points.                                                                       |
| **Verenis** | **Ivory Majesty:** Enemies within 15 feet who start their turn must make a Wisdom save or be Frightened until the start of their next turn.                                             |
| **Mornael** | **Storm-Frenzy:** When it reduces a creature to 0 hit points, it can immediately use its Reaction to move up to its speed towards another enemy without provoking opportunity attacks.  |
| **Aeloria** | **Sanguine Lust:** Its attacks that deal slashing or piercing damage deal an additional 1d4 necrotic damage. The werekin gains temporary hit points equal to the necrotic damage dealt. |
### Combo Mutations

When two or more moons contribute _equal intensity_ (e.g., both at 0.5x, or both at 1.0x), they may create a more powerful, fused effect that replaces the individual ones.

| Moons                                         | Combo Mutation & Effect                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| --------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Lunara + Verenis (The Crowned Hunter)**     | **Royal Decree:** As a Bonus Action, the werekin lets out a terrifying howl. One creature it can see must make a Wisdom save. On a failure, the creature is both Frightened and Paralyzed until the end of its next turn, stunned by majestic terror. |
| **Selthar + Aeloria (The Blood Bloom)**       | **Thorned Flesh:** The werekin's skin becomes like bark and thorns. Any creature that hits it with a melee attack takes 1d4 piercing + 1d4 necrotic damage. Its Verdant Regeneration now works anywhere and heals for 1d8.                            |
| **Mornael + Aeloria (The Crimson Typhoon)**   | **Chain of Carnage:** The bonus movement from Storm-Frenzy is now a teleport to an unoccupied space it can see within 30 feet. After teleporting, it can make a single claw attack against a creature within reach as part of the same Reaction.      |
| **Verenis + Mornael (The Sovereign's Wrath)** | **Awe and Ruin:** The range of Ivory Majesty increases to 30 feet. When a creature fails its save against Ivory Majesty, the werekin can use its Reaction to make a single attack against that creature.                                              |
| **Lunara + Selthar (The Ghost in the Woods)** | **Unseen Predator:** When the werekin gains advantage from Predatory Focus, it also becomes Invisible until the start of its next turn or until it makes an attack.                                                                                   |

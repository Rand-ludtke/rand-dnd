### **1. Recall Button**

**Name:** **Button of Summoned Serenity**  
**Wondrous Item, uncommon (requires attunement by a spellcaster with a familiar)**

This small, intricately designed button can be pressed as a bonus action. When activated, the familiar immediately returns to the caster’s side in an unoccupied space within 5 feet of them, regardless of distance or obstacles consuming 1 charge. Alternatively For 3 charges the caster can switch places with the familiar. The button has three charges and regains 1d4-1 expended charges daily at dawn.

### **2. Familiar's Shield**

**Name:** **Charm of the Guardian Spirit**  
**Wondrous Item, uncommon**

This tiny charm, often shaped like a shield or amulet, enhances a familiar’s resilience. While the familiar is within 100 feet of the caster and in active service:

- The familiar’s AC increases by 2.
- It gains temporary hit points equal to the caster’s level each day at dawn.

When the familiar drops to 0 hit points, the charm releases a burst of protective energy, allowing the caster to immediately recast _find familiar_ without expending a spell slot. This effect can only occur once per day.

### **3. Transformation Token**

**Name:** **Token of Majestic Beasts**  
**Wondrous Item, rare (requires attunement by a spellcaster with a familiar)**

This ornate token is adorned with carvings of fantastical creatures. Once per long rest, the caster can activate the token as an action, causing their familiar to temporarily transform into a more powerful creature for 1 minute.

- Choose a beast or monstrosity with a Challenge Rating up to Casters Level/4 rounded down(or higher at DM discretion).
- The familiar gains the new form’s stats, abilities, and movement, while retaining its intelligence and loyalty.

After the transformation ends, the familiar is unsummoned and cant be resummoned for 2d12 hours(or longer at DM discretion) and it cannot transform again until the next dawn.

### **Bonus Idea: Collar of Arcane Echoes**

**Name:** **Collar of Arcane Echoes**  
**Wondrous Item, uncommon (requires attunement by a spellcaster with a familiar)**

A magical collar imbued with a faint glow, it bonds with a familiar to enhance its connection with its caster. While wearing the collar, the familiar gains the following benefits:

- Twice per day the Familiar can Cast any known spell from the Spellcaster
- The familiar’s attacks count as magical for the purpose of overcoming resistance and immunity to nonmagical attacks and damage.

The collar recharges its magical properties at dawn.

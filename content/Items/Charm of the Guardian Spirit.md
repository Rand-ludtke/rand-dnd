**Wondrous Item, uncommon**

This tiny charm, often shaped like a shield or amulet, enhances a familiar’s resilience. While the familiar is within 100 feet of the caster and in active service:

- The familiar’s AC increases by 2.
- It gains temporary hit points equal to the caster’s level each day at dawn.

When the familiar drops to 0 hit points, the charm releases a burst of protective energy, allowing the caster to immediately recast _find familiar_ without expending a spell slot. This effect can only occur once per day.

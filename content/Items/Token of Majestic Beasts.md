**Wondrous Item, rare (requires attunement by a spellcaster with a familiar)**

This ornate token is adorned with carvings of fantastical creatures. Once per long rest, the caster can activate the token as an action, causing their familiar to temporarily transform into a more powerful creature for 1 minute.

- Choose a beast or monstrosity with a Challenge Rating up to Casters Level/4 rounded down(or higher at DM discretion).
- The familiar gains the new form’s stats, abilities, and movement, while retaining its intelligence and loyalty.

After the transformation ends, the familiar is unsummoned and cant be resummoned for 2d12 hours(or longer at DM discretion) and it cannot transform again until the next dawn.
